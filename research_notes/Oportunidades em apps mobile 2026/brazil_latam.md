## 1. Brazil app market size 2024-2026 (spend, downloads rank, iOS vs Android, ARPU, growth) and Mexico/LATAM comparison

### Takeaway
Brazil is a top-3 download market worldwide but a mid-size revenue market: roughly US$2.5B of in-app purchase revenue in 2025 (Mexico ~US$1.9B), growing fast (+29% YoY in Q3 2025, fastest of the top-20 markets). iOS is a minority of devices (roughly 20-27% depending on source) yet the Apple ecosystem study shows Brazilian developers already earn more than half of their App Store income from outside Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- Brazil and Mexico ranked 3rd and 6th globally for app downloads in 2025; IAP revenue reached about US$2.5B (Brazil) and US$1.9B (Mexico) in 2025. Secondhand: from a "LATAM App Trends Spotlight Report 2026" PDF (dated 22 Jun 2026) that could not be opened; publisher not verified — [LATAM App Trends Spotlight Report 2026 (PDF mirror)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-06-22-latam-app-trends-spotlight-report-2026_wp.pdf)
- LATAM 2025: app installs grew 13% and finance-app sessions grew 62% (headline of coverage of the same report family) — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/latam-mobile-apps-finance-sessions-surge-62-installs-grow-13-in-2025/)
- Q3 2025: Brazil recorded a 29% YoY increase in IAP revenue, the fastest among the top 20 global markets — [Sensor Tower Q3 2025 Digital Market Index (PDF mirror)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sensor_tower__q3_2025_digital_market_index.pdf)
- Q2 2025: Europe and Latin America were the key growth regions for non-game apps, with IAP revenue up more than 20% YoY in both; mobile-game IAP grew 6.2% YoY in Latin America (8.5% in Europe) — [Sensor Tower Q2 2025 Digital Market Index](https://sensortower.com/blog/q2-2025-digital-market-index)
- Apple-commissioned study by Prof. Silvia Fagá de Almeida (FGV), released Sep 2025: the App Store ecosystem facilitated ~R$63.8B in Brazil in 2024 — R$50.22B (79%) physical goods and services, R$7.46B (12%) in-app advertising, R$6.10B (10%) digital goods and services — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/loja-virtual-da-apple-movimenta-r-638-bilhoes-por-ano-no-brasil/); [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/04/09/2025/app-store-faturamento-br/)
- Same study: Brazilian users made ~1.5B App Store downloads in 2024; the store averaged 25.5M weekly visitors in Brazil; apps by Brazilian developers were downloaded 570M times by users abroad; **53% of Brazilian developers' earnings came from customers outside Brazil**; small developers' total earnings grew 55% between 2021 and 2024; Apple charges commission on only ~10% of ecosystem transactions and 78% of those pay the reduced ~15% rate — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/loja-virtual-da-apple-movimenta-r-638-bilhoes-por-ano-no-brasil/); [TecMundo](https://www.tecmundo.com.br/mercado/406935-app-store-gerou-renda-de-r-638-bilhoes-para-desenvolvedores-brasileiros-em-2024.htm)
- The same total is quoted as ~US$11.7B (implying ~R$5.45/US$) — [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/apple-app-store-generates-11-7b-in-brazil-revenue-supports-200k-jobs-amid-sideloading-push)
- Device/OS share in Brazil varies by method: StatCounter pages show Android ~75-79% and iOS ~21-25% in 2026 readings — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/brazil/2025); the Mobile Time/Opinion Box survey (2025, n=2,039) found Android on 72% of handsets and iOS on 27% — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/smartphone/samsung-cresce-mais-no-brasil-apple-e-motorola-ficam-para-tras-indica-pesquisa/); AppsFlyer puts Android at ~82% — [AppsFlyer Brazil app marketing report](https://www.appsflyer.com/pt/resources/reports/report-app-marketing-brazil/); one May 2025 reading reported Android 88.33% / iOS 11.55% — [Oficina da Net](https://www.oficinadanet.com.br/smartphones/62204-market-share-celulares-maio-2025-brasil). Sources conflict; the plausible range for iOS is ~12-27% of active devices.
- User-acquisition spend in Brazil was ~US$2.85B, 93% of it on Android; in 2024 iOS UA spend grew 168% while Android fell 22%; finance-app installs on iOS grew 60% YoY; about 50% of UA investment in Brazil comes from Chinese apps — [AppsFlyer Brazil app marketing report](https://www.appsflyer.com/pt/resources/reports/report-app-marketing-brazil/)
- Entertainment was the top-grossing app category in Brazil in H1 2024 at just over US$61M (Apptica data) — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1497189/top-brazil-app-categories-by-revenue)
- Apple reports Brazilian storefront sales in BRL, Mexico in MXN, Chile CLP, Colombia COP, Peru PEN; the rest of "Latin America and the Caribbean" (incl. Argentina, Ecuador, Uruguay) is a single USD-denominated region — [Apple, Financial report regions and currencies](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/financial-report-regions-and-currencies)

### Inferences
- Rough per-capita IAP spend (using the secondhand 2025 figures and ~213M / ~130M population): Brazil ≈ US$12 per person per year, Mexico ≈ US$15. Mexico monetizes better per head even though Brazil is the larger absolute market. No sourced US/EU ARPU figure was retrieved this session for a like-for-like comparison.
- R$6.10B of iOS "digital goods and services" in 2024 is ≈ US$1.1B at the study's implied rate. Set against ~US$2.5B total Brazil IAP (2025, both stores), this suggests iOS generates a share of digital spend far above its 12-27% device share. Caveat: the two numbers come from different sources, years and definitions (the FGV figure includes digital sales not billed through IAP), so this is directional only.
- The 53%-from-abroad figure and 570M foreign downloads are the strongest hard evidence found that Brazilian developers on iOS are already export-oriented on average.
- Half of Brazilian UA spend coming from Chinese apps signals heavy paid-acquisition competition in mass-market categories (shopping, short drama, games, finance).

### Gaps
- No directly verified Sensor Tower / data.ai / Appfigures figure for total Brazil consumer spend split by App Store vs Google Play, nor the iOS share of revenue. The Sensor Tower "State of Mobile 2026" was not reachable.
- iPhone installed base in units (millions of active iPhones in Brazil) not found.
- ARPU/ARPPU versus US and Europe: no Brazil-specific figure found (see Q4 for regional LTV proxies).
- Headline figures for Argentina, Colombia, Chile and LATAM total were not retrieved. A forecast seen in a search summary (Brazil app market US$8.1B by 2029; Mexico US$4.1B) could not be attributed to a specific source and is not relied on; Statista market-outlook pages exist but were not opened ([Statista Brazil](https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/app/brazil/), [Statista LATAM](https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/app/latam/)).

## 2. Top-grossing / top-downloaded apps on the Brazilian App Store and what they say about local demand; how global subscription apps price in BRL

### Takeaway
Hard chart data for the Brazilian App Store could not be pulled this session. The fragments available point to entertainment/video (TikTok, streaming, short drama) as the money category, Brazil as one of the largest short-drama audiences in the world by downloads, and very modest revenue for purely local utility apps (a leading Brazilian personal-finance app grosses about US$3K per week on Android).

### Cited Findings
- TikTok in Brazil, Q3 2025: weekly downloads peaked at ~1.5M in early September and weekly IAP revenue reached ~US$546K; Grindr averaged ~19K weekly downloads — [Sensor Tower, Brazil social apps Q3 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-unified-top-5-social%20apps-revenue-br-6009e912241bc16eb8566eef)
- Moises (AI music practice app) appears in Brazil's top-5 music apps by revenue: download peak ~57K/week in late May 2025 with weekly revenue near US$40K, and ~US$50K/week in early Dec 2025; the top music app's weekly active users stayed above 52M — [Sensor Tower, Brazil music & audio Q2 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-music%20and%20audio-revenue-br-6013bc4c241bc16eb8396743); [Sensor Tower, Q4 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-music-and-audio-revenue-br-6013bc4c241bc16eb8396743)
- Organizze (Brazilian personal-finance app) was in the Android consumer-finance top-5 by revenue in Brazil in Q3 2025 with only ~US$3K/week, and weekly downloads falling from 5.7K to 4.3K — [Sensor Tower, Brazil consumer finance Q3 2025 (Android)](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-consumer%20finance-revenue-br-63dd47dee1714cfff1d1c6df)
- A leading Android shopping app in Brazil peaked above 340K weekly downloads in late Jan 2025 and had nearly 29M active users by March 2025 — [Sensor Tower, Brazil shopping Q1 2025 (Android)](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-android-top-5-shopping-revenue-br-6571f22de1714cfff1c79b4a)
- Short drama globally: US$2.98B IAP revenue in 2025 (+115% YoY); DramaBox and ReelShort each made close to US$140M in Q1 2026 — [Sensor Tower, State of Short Drama Apps 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report)
- Brazil's weight in short drama: in May 2025 ReelShort had ~15M downloads, with Brazil the #1 country at 21.96% (US 9.29%, Mexico 7.74%) — [Vitrina](https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/); a monthly third-party tracker shows one top short-drama app with 40.61% of downloads and 45.83% of revenue from Brazil (single month, single app, unverified tracker) — [Insightrackr](https://blog.insightrackr.com/en/docs/Apr-2026-Top-Short-Drama)
- Chinese mini-dramas are reported as reshaping the streaming market in Latin America — [Times Brasil/CNBC](https://timesbrasil.com.br/entretenimento/na-america-latina-mini-dramas-chineses-estao-revolucionando-o-mercado-de-streaming/)
- Subscription habits (Vindi + Opinion Box survey): in 2025, 73% of Brazilian respondents subscribed to video streaming, 45% to music streaming and 40% to food-app benefit plans — [Mercado & Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/20/08/2025/servicos/servicos-de-streaming-lideram-modelo-de-assinaturas-no-brasil/); nearly 50% of Brazilians spend more than R$100 per month on subscriptions — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/mercado/quase-50-dos-brasileiros-gastam-mais-de-r-100-com-assinaturas-por-mes/)
- Historic willingness-to-pay baseline (Panorama Mobile Time/Opinion Box): in 2017, 56% of Brazilian smartphone users had ever made an in-app purchase — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/20/12/2017/56-dos-brasileiros-com-smartphone-ja-fizeram-compras-in-app/); in Dec 2023, 66% of smartphone owners subscribed to a streaming service — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/22/12/2023/66-dos-brasileiros-com-smartphone-assinam-servicos-de-streamings/)
- Fixed-odds betting apps can now be on the Brazilian App Store only with an SPA licence (see Q5) — [Apple Developer News, 8 May 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=x4eyetnp)
- Live Brazil top-grossing charts are published by third-party trackers (not opened) — [AppLaunchFlow BR top grossing](https://www.applaunchflow.com/tools/top-grossing/br); [42matters](https://42matters.com/top-grossing-mobile-apps-brazil)

### Inferences
- Brazil over-indexes on downloads/attention relative to revenue: it is the #1 download country for ReelShort yet short-drama revenue is led by the US. Formats that monetize by micro-payments/coins and ads fit Brazil better than high-priced annual plans.
- The Organizze data point (a well-known local finance app grossing ~US$12K/month on Android IAP) is a warning about pure Brazil-only utility subscriptions: reach can be large while store revenue stays small.
- Moises shows the opposite path: a product with global appeal that also charts in Brazil, earning ~US$40-50K/week in Brazil alone.

### Gaps
- Actual top-grossing and top-free lists for the Brazilian App Store by category (Nov 2026) were not retrieved; chart trackers were unreachable.
- No verified examples of BRL price points for global subscription apps (e.g. Duolingo, Tinder, ChatGPT, CapCut, Calm, Flo) versus their US prices. This needs a manual check of App Store Brazil listings.
- No sourced data on Bible/religion, dating, AI assistant, fitness, English-learning or ENEM/concurso apps' rank or revenue in Brazil.

## 3. Brazil-specific niches with demand and weak local competition (and evidence of indie successes)

### Takeaway
Only the CNH (driver's licence) niche could be documented this session: the 2025-2026 reform made driving school optional and moved theory study to a free official app, while candidate demand reportedly tripled. For the other niches (concursos/ENEM/OAB, MEI/nota fiscal, Imposto de Renda, FGTS/INSS, religion, Cartola, FIPE/multas, WhatsApp utilities, apostas-adjacent tools) no sized evidence was gathered and they remain hypotheses to validate.

### Cited Findings
- Contran approved new CNH rules on 1 Dec 2025 making driving-school classes optional — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/12/01/carros-e-tecnologia/cnh-mais-barata-e-rapida-aulas-em-autoescola-deixarao-de-ser-obrigatorias/)
- The rules are in force nationally since January 2026 for categories A and B; the theory course became free, online and without minimum hours, delivered through the official "CNH do Brasil" app; some state Detrans were still adapting their systems — [Correio](https://www.correio24horas.com.br/politica/cnh-mais-barata-em-2026-veja-o-que-mudou-e-o-que-voce-ainda-precisa-pagar-para-tirar-a-carteira-0526)
- Senatran published the Manual Brasileiro de Exames de Direção Veicular in Feb 2026 to standardize practical exams — [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/geral/noticia/2026-02/saiba-mais-sobre-novas-regras-nacionais-para-tirar-cnh)
- In May 2026 the government launched a platform (within the app) for finding a CFC or an accredited independent instructor — [O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/brasil/2026/05/06/governo-lanca-plataforma-para-facilitar-obtencao-da-cnh.html)
- Senatran formally notified the Detrans of Santa Catarina and Ceará for not adopting the new model — [Correio](https://www.correio24horas.com.br/brasil/cnh-sem-autoescola-governo-notifica-dois-estados-por-descumprirem-regras-0126)
- Demand for the CNH reportedly tripled after the end of mandatory driving school (headline; figures not read) — [AutoPapo](https://autopapo.com.br/curta/fim-da-autoescola-obrigatoria-triplica-a-procura-por-cnh-no-brasil-veja-os-numeros/)
- MEI utilities exist on the Brazilian App Store both as an official app and third-party apps, e.g. "MEI" and "MEI Digital: DAS e Abrir MEI" — [App Store: MEI](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/mei/id1040521803); [App Store: MEI Digital](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/id6755792228)
- Local personal-finance apps monetize weakly through the stores (Organizze ~US$3K/week on Android, Q3 2025) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-consumer%20finance-revenue-br-63dd47dee1714cfff1d1c6df)
- Apps with real-money fixed-odds betting need an SPA licence and get an A18 rating in Brazil; apps declaring loot boxes are rated 18+ in Brazil and are blocked for users not confirmed as adults — [Apple Developer News, 8 May 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=x4eyetnp); [Apple Developer News, 24 Feb 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)

### Inferences
- CNH: the state now gives away the theory course in an official app, which removes the "course" business but not the exam. With no mandatory hours and reportedly ~3x more candidates, self-study aids (simulados with the official question bank, spaced repetition, explanations) plausibly gain demand; the risk is that the official app adds simulators and that the niche is already crowded with ad-funded Android apps.
- Niches that depend on a government service (MEI/DAS, FGTS, INSS, Imposto de Renda, multas) share a structural risk: the government ships its own free app and can change rules or APIs, so third-party value must sit in workflow/UX layers rather than in access to the service.
- Betting-adjacent tools (tips, odds, bankroll trackers) carry elevated platform and legal risk after the SPA licensing rule and ECA Digital; anything that looks like facilitating betting for minors is a removal risk.

### Gaps
- No market sizing, competitor mapping or revenue evidence found for: concursos/ENEM/OAB prep, MEI invoicing (NFS-e/Pix/DAS), Imposto de Renda helpers, FGTS/INSS utilities, evangelical/Catholic apps, Cartola/fantasy football, horoscope, beauty, Brazilian-food diet apps, English learning, pets, FIPE/multas, creator tools, WhatsApp stickers/status tools.
- No verified indie success stories in Brazil with public revenue numbers were found (search budget ended before this query ran).
- **[background, unverified]** Leads worth checking: the national NFS-e standard becoming mandatory during the tax reform creates demand for MEI/ME invoicing tools; WhatsApp-dependent utilities have a history of takedowns for policy violations; exam-prep incumbents in Brazil (e.g. Qconcursos, Gran, Estratégia, Descomplica) are large web-first companies, so "weak competition" there is doubtful and should be tested per sub-niche (e.g. specific exam boards, OAB first phase, CNH, ENCCEJA).

## 4. Payments and pricing in Brazil (BRL tiers, purchasing-power pricing, Pix, instalments, conversion, refunds)

### Takeaway
Brazil is priced close to the US by default (Adapty's 2026 index: 0.9x), carries extra transaction taxes that Apple nets from proceeds (IOF 3.5% since Aug 2025), and prefers low-commitment plans (weekly plans are 29% of LATAM subscription revenue). Pix exists on Google Play only for one-off purchases; no evidence was found of Pix as an App Store payment method, although since iOS 26.5 developers may offer their own payment methods in Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- App Store pricing mechanics: you set a price on a base storefront and Apple generates equalized prices for the other 174 storefronts across 43 currencies, accounting for FX and certain taxes; 800 price points by default (+100 higher on request); you can override any storefront manually, after which Apple stops auto-adjusting it — [Apple, Set a price](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/)
- Apple's periodic tax/price updates exclude auto-renewable subscriptions and storefronts where you manage prices manually — [Apple Developer News, 11 May 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=y3w8ck7j)
- Since 21 Aug 2025 Apple modifies developer proceeds on Brazilian sales to account for IOF (Imposto sobre Operações Financeiras) of 3.5% — [Apple Developer News, 21 Aug 2025](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yo2104n5); local coverage: [MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/08/21/app-store-passa-a-recolher-35-de-iof-sobre-compras-e-assinaturas-no-brasil/)
- Since 16 May 2025 Apple modifies Brazilian proceeds for a 10% CIDE for **developers based outside Brazil**, and repriced Brazil from 2 Jun 2025 where Brazil is not the base storefront — [Apple Developer News, 16 May 2025](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=wim4cztw)
- Adapty 2026 benchmark: Brazil's subscription pricing index is 0.9x the US baseline (same as Argentina and UAE); LTV in top Latin American markets is ~35% below European ones — [PPC Land on Adapty 2026](https://ppc.land/95-of-app-subscription-revenue-goes-to-top-10-adaptys-2026-benchmark-report/)
- Weekly plans account for 29% of subscription revenue in Latin America (Adapty) — [The SourceCode](https://www.the-sourcecode.com/business-tech/subscription-app-market-shift-android-emerging-markets-strategy-2026)
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 (115K+ apps, US$16B): median year-one realized LTV per payer is US$32 in North America, US$25 in Western Europe and US$23 globally; no LATAM/Brazil figure surfaced — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/)
- Vendor claim (pricing-tool seller, not independently verified): apps with PPP-adjusted prices in 25+ territories earn 1.4-2.1x more revenue per impression than uniform USD-converted pricing; illustrative example of a US$9.99 price converting 2-4% in Brazil versus 8-14% near US$3.49 — [AppsOps](https://appsops.store/news/subscription-pricing-across-borders-revenuecat-confirms-indies); [AppsOps](https://appsops.store/news/subscription-pricing-growth-markets-roi-lever-2026)
- Google Play added Pix in Brazil on 6 Mar 2024, for one-time purchases only; subscriptions still need another method — [Google Brasil blog](https://blog.google/intl/pt-br/produtos/android-chrome-play/google-play-pix-chega-a-loja-de-apps-e-amplia-opcoes-de-pagamento/); [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/google-play-agora-aceita-pix-para-pagar-apps-e-games/)
- Pix Automático (recurring Pix) is mandatory since 1 Jan 2026 for institutions that offered interbank direct debit — [Band Vale](https://www.band.com.br/band-vale/noticias/como-o-pix-mudou-a-forma-que-o-brasileiro-paga-por-games-e-streaming-202607271556)
- The CADE-Apple settlement does not cover Pix in Apple Pay/NFC; that discussion continues with the Central Bank and CADE — [Jornal de Brasília](https://jornaldebrasilia.com.br/noticias/economia/apple-libera-sistema-de-pagamentos-em-aplicativos-no-brasil-apos-acordo-com-o-cade/); tap-to-pay Pix reached one year with weak numbers and an "Apple block" — [Finsiders Brasil](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/reportagem-exclusiva-fintechs/pix-por-aproximacao-faz-um-ano-com-numeros-timidos-e-trava-da-apple/)
- From iOS 26.5 developers in Brazil may process digital-goods payments outside Apple IAP (shown side by side with Apple IAP) or link out to the web, and are then responsible for collecting and remitting taxes on those sales — [Apple, Alterações no iOS no Brasil](https://developer.apple.com/br/support/app-distribution-in-brazil/)
- A report covered by MacMagazine (13 Oct 2025) says the App Store has almost 2x fewer subscription billing errors than Google Play — [MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/10/13/app-store-tem-quase-2x-menos-erros-com-assinaturas-do-que-o-google-play/)

### Inferences
- With a default index of 0.9x, Brazilians are being asked near-US prices on roughly one-fifth of US income; a manual BRL price well below the auto-equalized tier (and a weekly or monthly entry plan) is a low-cost experiment, since manual storefront prices are fully supported. Evidence for the size of the uplift is vendor-sourced and should be A/B tested, not assumed.
- The CADE settlement is the first practical route to Pix (including Pix Automático) for iOS digital goods in Brazil, via an alternative in-app processor or link-out — at the cost of handling tax, invoices, refunds and under-18 parental gates yourself.
- Because IOF (3.5%) is netted from proceeds, effective take on Brazilian iOS sales is lower than the headline commission implies.

### Gaps
- Actual BRL price ladder and typical BRL prices for weekly/monthly/annual plans versus US were not collected.
- Whether the 3.5% IOF adjustment applies to Brazil-based developers or only to foreign ones is not stated on Apple's page.
- No sourced data on instalment (parcelamento) behaviour inside app stores, trial-to-paid conversion in Brazil vs US, or refund/chargeback rates by country.
- Whether Apple Account in Brazil accepts Pix/boleto as a funding method was not confirmed (no source found either way).

## 5. Regulatory changes (CADE v. Apple, digital markets bill, ECA Digital, LGPD, bets, AI bill, consumer law)

### Takeaway
Two changes are already live and material for an iOS publisher in Brazil: (1) the CADE settlement, implemented with iOS 26.5 in June 2026, which cut Apple's Brazilian commission structure to 21%/10% plus a 5% Apple-IAP processing fee and allows alternative payments, link-outs and alternative marketplaces; and (2) ECA Digital (Law 15.211/2025, in force 17 Mar 2026), which makes app stores verify age, share an age signal with apps and bans relying on self-declared age. Apple has layered Brazil-specific age ratings and a betting-licence gate on top.

### Cited Findings
**CADE v. Apple**
- CADE approved a cease-and-desist agreement (TCC) with Apple on 23 Dec 2025, ending a roughly three-year investigation; Apple had up to 105 days to implement and the obligations last three years — [CADE (gov.br)](https://www.gov.br/cade/en/matters/news/cade-signs-a-cease-and-desist-agreement-with-apple); [MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/12/23/apple-permitira-compras-externas-e-lojas-alternativas-a-app-store-no-brasil/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-brazil-cade-sideloading-alternative-app-stores)
- Non-compliance fine reported at up to R$150M — [IT Forum](https://itforum.com.br/?p=9615238)
- Apple announced the implementation on 18 Jun 2026: from iOS 26.5 developers can distribute through alternative app marketplaces, operate marketplaces and process payments for digital goods outside Apple IAP; all Developer Program members had to accept the updated licence agreement by 6 Jul 2026 — [Apple Developer News, 18 Jun 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=dhwadr2x); [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/06/18/apple-announces-ios-app-store-changes-in-brazil)
- Brazil commission table (read directly): App Store commission 21% standard, or 10% for Small Business Program / Mini Apps / Video / News Partner Program members and for auto-renewing subscriptions after year one; separate 5% payment-processing fee when Apple IAP is used; link-out "services commission" 15% (10% for the reduced-rate groups) on sales completed within 7 days of the tap; 5% Core Technology Commission for apps distributed via alternative marketplaces — [Apple, Alterações no iOS no Brasil](https://developer.apple.com/br/support/app-distribution-in-brazil/)
- Conditions (read directly): Apple IAP must be offered alongside any alternative method; monthly transaction reporting within 15 days of month-end; the developer collects and remits taxes on alternative-payment sales; Kids-category apps cannot link out and need a parental gate; for under-18 users alternative in-app payments must sit behind parental verification and link-out purchases are not allowed — [Apple, Alterações no iOS no Brasil](https://developer.apple.com/br/support/app-distribution-in-brazil/)
- The final deal does not require open web sideloading; apps outside the App Store must come through an authorized alternative marketplace and pass Apple notarization — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-brazil-cade-sideloading-alternative-app-stores); [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=dhwadr2x)
- An earlier draft reportedly had a 25% base rate; the final terms are 21% — [Neon](https://www.neonpay.com/blog/apples-brazil-antitrust-settlement-whats-going-to-change-for-mobile-game-developers); [Appcharge](https://appcharge.com/blog/apple-brazil-framework-is-now-live-what-publishers-need-to-know)
- Critical commentary argues the settlement amounts to detailed ex-ante-style price regulation through an antitrust case — [Truth on the Market, 4 Feb 2026](https://truthonthemarket.com/2026/02/04/apple-in-brazil-ex-post-antitrust-meets-ex-ante-ambitions/)

**ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025)**
- Sanctioned 17 Sep 2025 (one source says 18 Sep) and in force since 17 Mar 2026; covers app stores and operating systems as well as social networks and games — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/03/17/internet-e-redes-sociais/eca-digital-entra-em-vigor-nesta-terca-saiba-o-que-muda-na-internet-brasileira/); [Machado Meyer factsheet](https://www.machadomeyer.com.br/images/ebooks/Factsheet_ECA_Digital.pdf)
- App stores/OS must take proportional, auditable measures to verify age or age range, provide an "age signal" via API to apps, obtain parental consent for minors' downloads and block betting apps lacking age verification; self-declaration of age is expressly not sufficient; developers are expected to use the age signal — [Demarest guide](https://www.demarest.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Cartilha-ECA-Digital_V2.pdf); [LegJur, Capítulo IV](https://www.legjur.com/legislacao/nivel/lei_00152112025/capitulo-iv-dos-mecanismos-de-afericao-de-idade); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/461777/verificacao-de-idade-obrigatoria-nos-games-e-agora)
- The law was regulated by decree and ANPD published age-verification guidance — [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/eca-digital-é-regulamentado-e-anpd-publica-orientações-sobre-verificação-etária)
- Apple's implementation (read directly): since 24 Feb 2026 the App Store blocks users in Brazil from downloading 18+ apps unless confirmed as adults; developers distributing in Brazil can use the Declared Age Range API to get the user's age category (shared when the user or guardian agrees); apps that declare loot boxes are rated 18+ on the Brazilian storefront; developers "may have separate obligations to independently confirm that their users are adults" — [Apple Developer News, 24 Feb 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)
- New age-rating descriptors (read directly): from 16 Nov 2026 the questionnaire adds "Shared Location" and "Dating and Relationships" (incl. romantic/sexual virtual companions); all apps must answer by 12 Jan 2027; in Brazil these carry minimum ratings of A16 and A18 respectively, with the option of requesting an official rating from the Ministry of Justice (MJSP) — [Apple Developer News, 2 Nov 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us)
- Brazil's self-rating ladder in App Store Connect is AL/A6/A10/A12/A14/A16/A18; e.g. advertising and messaging/chat sit at A12, social media and unrestricted web access at A16, gambling and loot boxes at A18 — [Apple, Age ratings values and definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions)

**Betting**
- Since 8 May 2026 apps with fixed-odds betting can be distributed on the Brazilian App Store only with a valid licence from the Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas (SPA), under Portaria SPA/MF 1.231 of 31 Jul 2024; answering "Yes" to gambling sets the Brazil rating to A18 and verification requires submitting a new app version — [Apple Developer News, 8 May 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=x4eyetnp)

**Tax-reporting clause**
- Apple's 13 Oct 2026 agreement update states Apple will report legally required information (tax, sales, contact details) to tax authorities — [Apple Developer News, 13 Oct 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=17mwd36z)

### Inferences
- For a Small Business Program member selling in Brazil via Apple IAP, the all-in Apple take is 10% + 5% = 15% (unchanged from before); for a non-member it is 21% + 5% = 26% instead of 30%. Using an alternative in-app processor leaves 10% (or 21%) to Apple plus the processor's cost; link-out costs 10% (or 15%) plus processor cost. For a small developer the saving versus Apple IAP is at most ~5 points minus PSP fees, in exchange for tax, invoicing, refund and reporting duties — probably only worth it to unlock Pix or instalments.
- ECA Digital plus Apple's rating rules raise the cost of categories that touch minors or adult themes: social features (A16), dating or AI companions (A18), loot boxes (18+), ads/chat (A12). An 18+ rating in Brazil shrinks the addressable audience to age-confirmed adults.
- The October 2026 reporting clause, together with the CNPJ requirement in Q6, suggests Apple is preparing to feed transaction data into the Brazilian tax-reform machinery; undeclared App Store income is increasingly visible to Receita Federal.

### Gaps
- LGPD specifics for apps, the digital-markets bill, the AI bill and consumer-law rules on subscriptions were not searched (budget exhausted). **[background, unverified]** leads: LGPD is Lei 13.709/2018 enforced by ANPD (privacy policy in Portuguese, legal basis, data-subject rights, DPO contact, children's data in the child's best interest); the fixed-odds betting framework is Lei 14.790/2023 with the regulated market starting Jan 2025; the government's digital-markets bill (PL 4.675/2025) would create a digital-markets unit inside CADE; the AI bill PL 2338/2023 passed the Senate in Dec 2024 and was pending in the Chamber; the Consumer Code (CDC art. 49) gives a 7-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases. Status of each as of Nov 2026 needs checking.
- The CADE TCC text itself and the gov.br press release could not be opened; details above come from Apple's own pages and press coverage.
- Whether any alternative marketplace is actually operating in Brazil yet was not established.

## 6. Practicalities for a Brazilian developer selling worldwide via Apple (enrolment, entity, payouts, tax)

> General information only — confirm every point with an accountant/lawyer. Rules changed during 2025-2026 and are still moving because of the tax reform.

### Takeaway
The biggest practical news is that Apple's App Store Connect help now lists a **CNPJ as the required tax information for Brazil-based developers** (CPF no longer mentioned), apparently changed around late October 2026 without notice, while Apple's contract exhibit still says "CNPJ or CPF". Apple treats sole proprietors as "individuals" for enrolment, and Brazilian sources say MEI is both unsuitable for software development and not accepted by Apple as an organization. On tax, sales to users abroad can qualify as service exports (ISS/PIS/COFINS relief), but a 2025 Receita ruling indicates revenue from Brazilian users is not an export under the Simples even when paid through a foreign platform.

### Cited Findings
**Enrolment**
- Individuals, sole proprietors and single-person businesses enrol as individuals; the personal legal name is shown as the App Store seller. Organizations need legal-entity status, a D-U-N-S Number, a work e-mail on the organization's domain and a real website; DBAs, fictitious names, trade names and branches are not accepted. Fee US$99 per year, shown in local currency — [Apple, Enroll](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/); [Apple, Enrollment support](https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/)
- Individuals pay at enrolment; organizations pay only after Apple verifies them; organizations may be asked for notarized business documents; individuals paying by card must use their own card or may be asked for government photo ID; converting an individual membership to an organization is done through Apple Developer Support — [Apple, Enrollment support](https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/)
- Brazilian community guidance: MEI, trade names and branches do not count as an organization for Apple, so a MEI enrols as an individual — [TabNews guide](https://www.tabnews.com.br/adevelop/inscricao-no-apple-developer-program-o-que-a-apple-realmente-exige-e-onde-as-pessoas-travam); a MEI CNPJ is not recognised automatically and needs contact with Apple support — [Ícone Estúdio guide](https://iconestudio.com.br/guias/como-publicar-aplicativo-na-app-store)
- A Brazilian accounting firm states a developer cannot be MEI for this activity — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/imposto-nota-fiscal-desenvolvedor)
- An anecdotal 2026 forum report describes a Brazilian individual enrolment stuck at the payment step — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/838891)

**CPF vs CNPJ (changed in 2026)**
- App Store Connect help, read 13 Nov 2026 — Brazil: "Tax forms required for: Developers based in Brazil. Required tax information: Cadastro Nacional de Pessoas Jurídicas (CNPJ), a 14 character corporate tax ID." CPF is not mentioned — [Apple, Tax forms overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/provide-tax-information/tax-forms-overview)
- Apple's contract exhibit (Portuguese, version dated 27 Aug 2026), clause 2.5: a developer resident in Brazil must update the account with the Brazilian taxpayer number "(CNPJ ou CPF, conforme o caso)" and, failing that, the apps "poderão ser removidos da Loja brasileira" until it is provided — [Apple, Exhibits to Schedules 2 and 3 (pt-BR PDF)](https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/exhibits/Exhibits-to-Schedule-2-and-3-Portuguese-Brazil.pdf)
- Brazilian tech press: until 21 Oct 2026 Apple's help accepted CPF or CNPJ; it now asks only for CNPJ; Apple published no notice, deadline or consequence; Receita Federal accepts CPF for individuals until the end of 2026 and the CNPJ obligation for individuals who are CBS taxpayers starts 1 Jan 2027 (the article cites "Decreto 13.075/2026" — single source, not verified) — [Antihype](https://antihype.com.br/c/software/app-store-brasil-cadastro-cpf-cnpj-desenvolvedor-apps-pagos/)
- Community report: some Brazilian developers received Apple notices asking them to replace CPF with CNPJ within 30 days, citing the tax reform; the author argues this will likely be a "CNPJ for individuals" under the reform rather than a company, with simplified registration expected around Nov 2026 — [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/marcelxsilva/a-apple-esta-pedindo-cnpj-no-lugar-do-cpf-para-continuar-pagando-voce-mas-calma)

**Contract structure and Brazilian-storefront taxes**
- Apple Services LATAM LLC acts as the developer's agent for Brazil, Mexico and the rest of Latin America; Apple Inc. for the US; Apple Distribution International Ltd. as commissionaire for Europe and most other regions — [Apple, Exhibits to Schedules 2 and 3 (pt-BR PDF)](https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/exhibits/Exhibits-to-Schedule-2-and-3-Portuguese-Brazil.pdf)
- Brazil clauses 2.1-2.3 (same exhibit): the developer is "o único responsável" for indirect taxes on delivery of its apps to end users in Brazil and for determining its own taxpayer status; Apple may use a "Entidade Arrecadadora" in Brazil to collect from users and remit abroad; where remittances out of Brazil are subject to withholding tax, that entity deducts it from the gross amount owed and pays it to the Brazilian tax authorities in the developer's name — [Apple, Exhibits (pt-BR PDF)](https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/exhibits/Exhibits-to-Schedule-2-and-3-Portuguese-Brazil.pdf)
- Apple began withholding taxes on all App Store sales in Brazil on 31 May 2023; coverage said Brazil-based developers were not affected — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/31/05/2023/apple-muda-regra-e-retera-impostos-em-vendas-na-app-store-de-desenvolvedores-estrangeiros-no-brasil/); [TecMundo](https://www.tecmundo.com.br/mercado/264697-apple-passa-reter-impostos-vendas-app-store-brasil.htm)
- Monthly withholding-tax reports for Brazil and an annual Brazilian withholding certificate are downloadable in App Store Connect — [Apple, Manage invoices and other tax documents](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/manage-invoices-and-other-tax-documents)
- Further Brazil-specific deductions from proceeds: IOF 3.5% (from 21 Aug 2025) and, for developers based outside Brazil, CIDE 10% (from 16 May 2025) — [Apple News, Aug 2025](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yo2104n5); [Apple News, May 2025](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=wim4cztw)
- A 2024 developer-forum thread shows the contract wording on Brazilian taxes is read differently by developers and received no official Apple answer — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/746074)

**Payouts**
- Apple pays within 45 days of the end of the fiscal month, once per currency per fiscal month, to the single primary bank account, in the currency of that account; Apple's bank does the conversion at roughly the spot rate; local and intermediary bank fees may be deducted; a minimum payment threshold applies — [Apple, Overview of receiving payments](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments)
- Google Play (for comparison): accounts denominated in BRL are paid in BRL to a bank account in Brazil and are not subject to the 25% withholding that applies to non-BRL payouts (rule described by Google since 2014; dated) — [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9384608)

**US tax form**
- Every developer must complete a US tax form for the Paid Apps Agreement; non-US developers are routed to W-8BEN (individuals), W-8BEN-E (entities) or W-8ECI — [Apple, Tax forms overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/provide-tax-information/tax-forms-overview)
- Brazil has no income-tax treaty with the US, so a Brazilian filer relies on unilateral relief — [WorkGlobal guide](https://www.workglobal.it.com/guides/brazilian-freelancer-us-company-tax-guide); by analogy (YouTube, not App Store) Receita lets US tax withheld be offset in the carnê-leão up to the Brazilian tax on that income — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/pt/monetization/youtube-tax-info-w-8ben)

**Small Business Program**
- 15% commission (10% on subscriptions after year one) for developers whose proceeds, together with associated accounts, were no more than US$1M in the prior calendar year and stay under US$1M in the current year; new developers qualify; you must enrol, and the rate applies from 15 days after the end of the fiscal month of approval; crossing US$1M moves you to the standard rate for the rest of the year — [Apple, App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/)
- In Brazil the equivalent post-settlement numbers are 10% commission + 5% Apple IAP fee — [Apple, Alterações no iOS no Brasil](https://developer.apple.com/br/support/app-distribution-in-brazil/)

**Brazilian taxation of the income (general explainer level)**
- Individuals: income received from sources abroad or from individuals is taxed monthly through carnê-leão, due by the last business day of the following month — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/imposto-nota-fiscal-desenvolvedor)
- Service exports: ISS does not apply to exported services, but does apply when the service is developed in Brazil and its result is verified in Brazil even if a foreign resident pays — [Econet](https://blog.econeteditora.com.br/o-que-e-exportacao-de-servicos)
- PIS/COFINS non-incidence under the Simples requires, cumulatively, a customer domiciled abroad and an inflow of foreign currency; issuing the NFS-e as an export is what removes ISS/PIS/COFINS from the DAS calculation — [Tributo Devido](https://tributodevido.com.br/portal/criterios-para-nao-incidencia-de-pis-cofins-na-exportacao-de-servicos-no-simples-nacional/); [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/?p=4340); [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/exportacao-de-servicos-no-simples-nacional/)
- Receita ruling SC Cosit 185/2025: content viewed in Brazil is not service-export revenue under the Simples Nacional even if payment is intermediated by a platform based abroad and brings in foreign currency — [SC Cosit 185/2025 (PDF)](https://rota.apet.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SC-Cosit-no-185-2025.pdf)
- Receita ruling SC Cosit 162/2026 (27 Aug 2026): helping market and promote a foreign party's software, paid by that foreign party, is treated as a service export for PIS/COFINS — [SC Cosit 162/2026 (PDF)](https://rotadajurisprudencia.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SC-Cosit-ny-162-2026.pdf); [Contadores.cnt.br](https://www.contadores.cnt.br/legislacoes/solucao-de-consulta-cosit-no-162-de-01-09-2026.html)

**Tax reform (IBS/CBS)**
- 2026 is the test year with symbolic rates (CBS 0.9% + IBS 0.1%) shown on invoices; effective collection starts in 2027 and the old taxes phase out by 2033 — [Company Hero](https://www.companyhero.com/blog/reforma-tributaria-cbs-ibs); [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/blog/gestao-contabil-e-fiscal/impactos-reforma-tributaria/)
- The Simples Nacional is preserved (LC 214/2025 and LC 227/2026), with an optional "hybrid" regime that pays IBS/CBS outside the DAS — [Contabilizei](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/contabilidade-online/reforma-tributaria-simples-nacional); [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/guias/simples-nacional-na-reforma-tributaria/)
- Exports are zero-rated for IBS and CBS regardless of the company's regime — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/blog/gestao-contabil-e-fiscal/exportacao-de-servicos); [Jornal Contábil](https://www.jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/por-que-a-reforma-tributaria-deve-impulsionar-as-exportacoes-de-tecnologia-no-brasil/)
- Sources disagree on whether Simples companies must show IBS/CBS on invoices in 2026 and on the date the national NFS-e becomes mandatory for them (Resolução CGSN 189/2026 is cited) — [Meu Contador Online](https://www.meucontadoronline.com.br/blog/reforma-tributaria-em-2026/); [Contabilizei](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/contabilidade-online/reforma-tributaria-simples-nacional)

### Inferences
- Treat "have a CNPJ before turning on paid apps/IAP" as the working assumption for late 2026 onward. Whether Apple will accept the reform's CNPJ-for-individuals, a MEI CNPJ, or only a company (e.g. SLU/LTDA under the Simples) is unresolved in the sources; a single-member limited company with a D-U-N-S would also allow enrolling as an organization (company name as seller) instead of the personal name.
- App Store revenue for a Brazil-based developer likely needs to be split for tax purposes: sales to users abroad (candidate for export treatment) versus sales to users in Brazil (domestic revenue, fully taxed), by analogy with SC Cosit 185/2025. Apple's per-storefront financial reports (Brazil in BRL, others in local currency/USD) make that split feasible. This is an inference from a ruling about content platforms, not a ruling about app stores — it needs an accountant's sign-off.
- Because Apple pays in the bank account's currency, the choice between a BRL account in Brazil and a USD account (abroad or multi-currency) determines who performs the FX and what IOF/spread applies; no source quantified this for Apple.

## 7. Global-first versus Brazil-first strategy; Brazilian studios and indies who succeeded globally

### Takeaway
The only hard evidence gathered favours export orientation: 53% of Brazilian developers' App Store earnings already come from abroad, LATAM subscriber LTV runs ~35% below Europe, and a leading local finance app earns only a few thousand dollars a week in store revenue. Brazil's strengths are volume and growth (top-3 downloads, +29% IAP growth), which suits it as a secondary, localized market or a launch test bed more than as the sole revenue base.

### Cited Findings
- 53% of Brazilian developers' App Store earnings in 2024 came from customers outside Brazil; Brazilian apps had 570M downloads abroad; small developers' earnings rose 55% from 2021 to 2024 — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/loja-virtual-da-apple-movimenta-r-638-bilhoes-por-ano-no-brasil/)
- LTV in top Latin American markets is ~35% below European ones while Brazil's price index is 0.9x the US — [PPC Land on Adapty 2026](https://ppc.land/95-of-app-subscription-revenue-goes-to-top-10-adaptys-2026-benchmark-report/)
- Median first-year LTV per payer: US$32 North America, US$25 Western Europe, US$23 global — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/)
- Brazil's IAP revenue grew 29% YoY in Q3 2025, fastest of the top 20 markets; Latin America non-game IAP grew over 20% YoY in Q2 2025 — [Sensor Tower Q3 2025 (PDF mirror)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sensor_tower__q3_2025_digital_market_index.pdf); [Sensor Tower Q2 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/q2-2025-digital-market-index)
- Moises earns roughly US$40-50K per week in Brazil alone while being a globally distributed app — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-music-and-audio-revenue-br-6013bc4c241bc16eb8396743)
- Organizze, a Brazil-only finance app, earns ~US$3K per week on Android — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-consumer%20finance-revenue-br-63dd47dee1714cfff1d1c6df)
- Brazil is the largest download market for ReelShort (21.96% of downloads in May 2025), illustrating how global publishers use Portuguese localization for volume — [Vitrina](https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/)
- Export revenue also has a tax advantage for a Brazilian company (ISS/PIS/COFINS relief; zero-rated under IBS/CBS) that domestic revenue does not — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/blog/gestao-contabil-e-fiscal/exportacao-de-servicos)

### Inferences
- A reasonable reading: build for a global (English-first) audience with Portuguese and Spanish localization and manual BRL/MXN pricing, rather than Brazil-only. Brazil-only makes sense where the product is inherently local (exams, tax, government services, local content) and has a non-store monetization path or very large volume.
- A Brazilian founder has a genuine edge in localizing proven global formats for Portuguese (native copy, cultural fit, local payment via the new alternative-payment rules), where foreign publishers often ship weak machine translation.
- Export revenue is taxed more lightly than domestic revenue for a Brazilian entity, which adds a financial reason to favour foreign storefronts on top of higher LTV.

## 8. Spanish-speaking LATAM and other emerging markets as low-CPI testing grounds

### Takeaway
LATAM installs cost roughly a quarter to a half of North American ones (about US$0.50-2.00 versus US$2-5+), which makes Brazil and Mexico usable for cheap funnel, retention and creative tests — but CPIs in the region rose ~37% in the year to Q2 2026, and low LTV means monetization results do not transfer to tier-1 markets.

### Cited Findings
- LATAM CPI roughly US$0.50-2.00 versus US$2-5 in North America (gaming context) — [Global Games Forum](https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/by-the-numbers-the-markets-driving-mobile-gamings-next-boom-in-2025)
- Rewarded-playtime campaigns on Android in Brazil typically cost US$0.41-1.00 per install; LATAM generally US$0.50-2.00 — [adjoe](https://adjoe.io/?p=117337)
- Non-gaming ranges: Brazil US$0.80-2.00, Mexico US$0.70-1.80, versus US$3.50-6.50 for non-gaming iOS apps in the US — [Semnexus](https://semnexus.com/cpi-by-country-where-app-budget-goes-furthest-2026/)
- Conflicting higher figure: median Facebook-ads cost per app install in Brazil ~US$5.84 over Jul 2025-Jun 2026, with iOS often US$2-5+ and Android US$1-3 — [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-app-install/brazil)
- Global benchmark Q2 2026: Android CPI US$0.76 (+15.1%), iOS CPI US$3.03 (-1.1%) — [Singular Quarterly Trends Report Q2 2026](https://www.singular.net/quarterly-report-2026-q2/)
- LatAm CPI rose 37% between Q2 2025 and Q2 2026, led by Colombia (+60%) and Mexico (+38%) (finance-marketing report, so likely finance-vertical weighted) — [AppsFlyer, State of Finance for Marketers LatAm 2026](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/finance-marketers-latam-report/)
- 93% of Brazil's UA spend goes to Android; iOS UA spend grew 168% in 2024 — [AppsFlyer Brazil report](https://www.appsflyer.com/pt/resources/reports/report-app-marketing-brazil/)
- Programmatic ad-revenue rankings for LATAM apps are published monthly by Pixalate (not opened) — [Pixalate](https://www.pixalate.com/blog/pixalates-august-2025-top-grossing-mobile-and-ctv-apps-in-latam)

### Inferences
- Pros: cheap volume for testing onboarding, retention, creatives and store-listing conversion; huge Android reach; Spanish covers ~20 storefronts with one localization.
- Cons: paywall conversion, price sensitivity and LTV differ from the US, so pricing/monetization tests do not generalize; ad eCPMs are lower; CPIs are rising quickly; on iOS specifically the LATAM audience is small and skewed to higher incomes.
- The wide spread between sources (US$0.41 to US$5.84 for Brazil) reflects channel, platform and optimization goal; a developer should budget a small pilot rather than rely on published averages.

### Gaps
- No sourced ad eCPM (rewarded/interstitial/banner) by country for Brazil, Mexico or the US.
- No iOS-specific CPI for Brazil/Mexico from a primary MMP report.
- No evidence on other emerging test markets (Philippines, India, Indonesia, Turkey) was collected.

## 9. Brazilian developer community and references

### Takeaway
The one verified asset is Apple's own footprint: Brazil hosts 10 Apple Developer Academy sites, the only ones in Latin America. Community groups, podcasts, newsletters, accelerators and publishers could not be researched this session.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Developer Academy locations in Brazil (10): Brasília (UCB), Campinas, Manaus and Porto Alegre (Instituto Eldorado), Curitiba (PUCPR), Fortaleza (IFCE), Recife (CIn-UFPE), Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), São Paulo (Mackenzie) and São Paulo (Senac); enrolment at the partner university/technical school is required; the curriculum is free; no other Latin American country is listed (partner names inferred from the site domains) — [Apple Developer Academy](https://developer.apple.com/academies/)
