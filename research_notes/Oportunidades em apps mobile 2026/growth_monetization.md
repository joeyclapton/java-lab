## 1. App Store Optimization in 2026 (ranking, new discovery features, tools)

### Takeaway
The dependable keyword levers are still title, subtitle and keyword field; the 2025-2026 additions (screenshot-caption text, custom product pages in organic search, AI-generated tags) are real product changes, but the ranking effect of captions is contested by the one independent test found. Organic search got structurally less valuable in March 2026, when Apple added more ad slots above organic results.

### Cited Findings
- **Screenshot captions (claimed since June 2025).** Several ASO guides say Apple began extracting screenshot caption text by OCR in June 2025 and treats it as a ranking signal; Appfigures published this as "The Biggest App Store Algorithm Change". — [Appfigures guide](https://appfigures.com/resources/guides/app-store-algorithm-update-2025); [appsops.store, 2026](https://appsops.store/news/custom-product-pages-organic-search-caption-ranking-2026)
- **Counter-evidence.** A ConsultMyApp / APPlyzer study tested 64 caption-derived phrases across 8 leading iOS apps and reported "no strong evidence Apple is broadly indexing screenshot titles". Apple has not confirmed the effect in documentation surfaced by the search. — [ConsultMyApp](https://consultmyapp.squarespace.com/blog/-is-apple-now-indexing-screenshot-titles-on-the-app-store); contradicts [Appfigures guide](https://appfigures.com/resources/guides/app-store-algorithm-update-2025)
- **Practical consensus in the guides:** keep the main keyword effort in title, subtitle and keyword field; align captions with real keywords because it is free and helps conversion; treat any ranking lift as a bonus. — [SplitMetrics ranking factors, 1 Sep 2025](https://splitmetrics.com/blog/apple-app-store-ranking-factors/); [Phiture, ASO trends in 2026](https://phiture.com/blog/aso-trends-in-2026/)
- **App Store tags (iOS 26).** Tags are generated by a language model and checked by an editorial reviewer; developers cannot pick tags, only ask for one to be removed; Apple has not said how often tags are regenerated. — [AppTweak WWDC 2025 recap](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-wwdc-2025-recap)
- **Tags and ranking (unproven).** One practitioner newsletter reports tags appear on contextual queries (for example "sports games") rather than broad ones ("sports") and argues they act as semantic ranking cues; this rests on the author's own before/after observations, not published data. — [Neoads Substack](https://neoads.substack.com/p/app-tags-appstore)
- **Custom product pages in organic search (iOS 26).** Custom product pages can appear in organic search results, and developers can link keywords from the app's keyword field to a specific custom page in App Store Connect. — [AppTweak WWDC 2025 recap](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-wwdc-2025-recap); [Appfigures video teardown](https://appfigures.com/resources/videos/screenshot-tips-proven-to-convert-live-product-page-teardown)
- **Measurement caveat.** Organic conversion from a custom page is hard to isolate because it also depends on rank position. — [AppTweak WWDC 2025 recap](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-wwdc-2025-recap)
- **Adoption (July 2025).** 83 of the top 100 grossing US apps and games use custom product pages. — [Appfigures, 18 Jul 2025](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250718/1--custom-product-pages-are-the-app-stores-secret-weapon)
- **Organic position diluted (March 2026).** With the extra ad slots, an app ranked first organically for a keyword may now appear below two ads. — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-search-results-are-expanding)
- **Tool prices (2026, third-party comparisons; several written by a competing vendor, Sonar).**
  - Astro: about $9/month billed annually ($108/year), single Mac licence, unlimited keywords, iOS-only, no API. — [Sonar on Astro pricing](https://trysonar.app/blog/astro-aso-pricing)
  - Appfigures: ASO-inclusive tier listed at $69/month; another guide gives an entry price of $9.99/month and a $44.99 "Monitor" plan as the practical entry for ASO work. These conflict. — [Sonar on Appfigures alternatives](https://trysonar.app/blog/best-appfigures-alternatives-for-aso); [AppFollow ASO cost guide](https://appfollow.io/blog/app-store-optimization-cost)
  - AppTweak: Essential plan listed at $79/month for 500 tracked keywords (API separate); another source lists from $58/month (annual) or $69/month (monthly) up to $549/month. — [Sonar on AppTweak alternatives](https://trysonar.app/blog/best-apptweak-alternatives-for-aso); [AppFollow ASO cost guide](https://appfollow.io/blog/app-store-optimization-cost)
  - ASOMobile: Indie plan $59/month for 500 keywords and 1,000 API credits. — [Sonar on AppTweak alternatives](https://trysonar.app/blog/best-apptweak-alternatives-for-aso)

### Inferences
- For a solo developer the cheapest defensible ASO stack in 2026 is one low-cost keyword tool (Astro-class, about $108/year) plus App Store Connect's own features (custom product pages linked to keywords, product page tests); the $60-80/month tools are only justified once keyword work is a weekly activity.
- Because custom pages can now be matched to keywords in organic search, the old "one screenshot set for everyone" approach leaves conversion on the table: an app can show a different first screenshot per keyword cluster at no cost.
- Caption indexing should be treated as a free hedge: write captions with real keywords, but do not move keywords out of title/subtitle on the strength of it.
- Tags cannot be controlled directly, so the only action is to keep metadata and screenshots unambiguous about the app's use case and to request removal of wrong tags.

### Gaps
- Share of installs coming from App Store search: no source retrieved. **[UNVERIFIED]** Apple's long-standing marketing claim is that roughly 65% of downloads occur directly after a search; check Apple Ads' site before quoting.
- Ratings velocity and conversion rate as ranking factors: named in the guide titles above but no numbers retrieved.
- Metadata localization as a growth lever (cost, measured uplift, cross-localization): no source retrieved.
- In-app events and product page optimization tests: no data retrieved. **[UNVERIFIED]** Product page tests allow up to 3 treatments for up to 90 days; indexed fields are app name (30 characters), subtitle (30) and keyword field (100); the custom product page cap was raised from 35 to 70 in 2025.
- Prices for Sensor Tower, AppFollow and FoxData: not retrieved.

## 2. Apple Search Ads (now "Apple Ads") in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Apple Ads remains the highest-intent paid channel but got more crowded: since 3 March 2026 search results carry more than one ad, positions cannot be chosen, and unchanged budgets can exhaust earlier in the day. Costs vary by an order of magnitude by category (about $1 CPA in Photo & Video versus $14-20 in Sports in 2025 data) and are lowest in Latin America and Africa/Middle East/India.

### Cited Findings
- **New slots.** Apple announced on 18 December 2025 that additional ads would appear in App Store search results in 2026. — [MacRumors, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/12/18/app-store-search-results-more-ads-2026/); [AppleInsider, 18 Dec 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/12/18/more-ads-are-coming-to-the-app-store-in-2026)
- **Timing and rollout.** The additional placements started on 3 March 2026, beginning in the UK and Japan before expanding. — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3041228/get-ready-for-more-ads-in-the-app-store.html); [Zoomd](https://www.zoomd.com/apple-to-expand-app-store-search-ads-from-march-2026-mobile-marketing-takeaways-for-app-advertisers/)
- **How many.** Up to two ads can appear for one query according to AppTweak; Apple itself only speaks of multiple positions including the existing top one and has not published a number. — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-search-results-are-expanding); [heise](https://heise.de/-11119517)
- **Mechanics.** Existing search results campaigns qualify without changes; advertisers cannot select or bid for a specific position; placement depends on bid and relevance. — [MacRumors, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/12/18/app-store-search-results-more-ads-2026/); [Mobile Action](https://www.mobileaction.co/blog/apple-ads-expanded-search-results-inventory/)
- **Budget effect.** If budgets stay unchanged, campaigns can hit daily caps earlier and stop serving later in the day. — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-search-results-are-expanding)
- **Small-developer reaction.** Coverage of the announcement quoted an App Store Award-winning developer calling the change "bad news" for smaller developers (exact outlet not confirmed from the summary). — [heise](https://heise.de/-11119517)
- **SplitMetrics benchmarks (2025 data, published 2026), search results placement.** Highest CPT (cost per tap): Sports $14.41, Finance $6.06, Medical $4.45. Sports also had the highest CPA (cost per acquisition/install) at $19.71. — [SplitMetrics report, executive summary](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/executive-summary)
- **Seasonality (SplitMetrics, 2025 data).** September was the most expensive month (CPT about $2.00, CPA about $3.20); April was the cheapest. — [SplitMetrics report, executive summary](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/executive-summary)
- **Regions (SplitMetrics, 2025 data).** North America is the highest-cost market; Latin America and Africa/Middle East/India are the cheapest and most stable for CPT and CPA. — [SplitMetrics report, markets](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/benchmarks-by-markets)
- **Mobile Action benchmarks (2025 data).** Sports CPA rose from $7.75 (2024) to $14.35 (2025); Photo & Video was the lowest-CPA category at $1.03. — [Mobile Action 2026 report](https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-ads-2026-benchmark-report/executive-summary/); differs from SplitMetrics' Sports CPA of $19.71 ([SplitMetrics](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/executive-summary))
- **AppTweak benchmarks (about 2,800 US advertisers, 2025 data).** US search results median CPT: Sports $14.56, Finance $4.17. — [AppTweak Apple Ads benchmarks](https://apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks)
- **Geo arbitrage for subscription apps (headline only).** A PPC Land article on Adapty's 2026 Apple Ads data is titled "Apple Ads gives Brazil 76 paid subscribers per 1,000, US gets 15". The denominator (per 1,000 installs or taps) was not visible. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/apple-ads-gives-brazil-76-paid-subscribers-per-1-000-us-gets-15/); underlying report: [Adapty Apple Ads benchmarks](https://adapty.io/apple-ads-for-subscription-apps/)
- **Install quality ranking.** One 2026 vendor source ranks iOS channels by install quality as Apple Ads first, then TikTok, Meta, Google (carrying page not confirmed). — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/tips/blog/mobile-app-acquisition-roi-beyond-cpi)

### Inferences
- The three benchmark vendors agree on direction (Sports and Finance are expensive, Photo & Video and utilities are cheap, September is the worst month to start) but not on absolute numbers; compare within one source only.
- For a small budget the cost data argues for starting outside the US or in cheaper English-speaking and Latin American storefronts, and for avoiding the September peak for tests.
- Brand defense matters more after March 2026: with two ads above organic results, a competitor can sit above an app's own brand term even when the app ranks first organically.
- The Brazil-versus-US headline, if the report bears it out, suggests paid subscribers per unit of traffic can be several times higher in cheaper markets, although revenue per subscriber is lower there (Adapty's price index puts India at 0.6x and Turkey at 0.7x of US prices; see section 6).

### Gaps
- CPT/CPA for Today tab, Search tab and product page placements: not retrieved. **[UNVERIFIED]** Apple Ads has four placements (Today tab, Search tab, search results, product pages while browsing) and was renamed from Apple Search Ads in 2025.
- Full per-category CPT/CPA tables and per-country tables: the reports exist (links above) but could not be opened.
- Concrete small-budget strategies (exact-match versus discovery campaign structure, daily budget floors, brand-defense cost): no source retrieved.
- Whether the extra slots raised or lowered average CPT after March 2026: no post-rollout data retrieved.

## 3. Organic short-video distribution (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), creators and platform risk

### Takeaway
The best-documented playbook (Cal AI) is paid seeding through hundreds of small creators on fixed retainers, targeting low single-digit dollars per thousand views, then adding paid ads once creator reach plateaus; nearly all numbers come from agency blogs and founder interviews, not audited data. TikTok is legally stable in the US since the joint venture closed in January 2026, but account-farm and slideshow tactics rest on vendor claims and carry terms-of-service and suppression risk.

### Cited Findings
- **Cal AI structure.** About 250 mid-sized and micro fitness/nutrition creators on fixed monthly retainers posting on TikTok and Instagram, with minimal creative direction (for example, show the app in the first 15 seconds). Carrying page among the agency write-ups not confirmed. — [FunnelFox blog](https://blog.funnelfox.com/cal-ai-influencer-marketing/); [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/cal-ai-brand-influencer-marketing-strategy/)
- **Cal AI spend and CPM target.** Influencer spend was reported at mid six figures per month in May 2025; a $5 CPM target is reported second-hand from an X Space, so it is weak evidence (carrying page for the CPM figure not confirmed). — [Latka founder interview, May 2025](https://getlatka.com/interviews/cal-ai-zach-yadegari-2025); [Operators Notebook](https://operatorsnotebook.beehiiv.com/p/the-ultimate-cheat-sheet-influencer-marketing-edition)
- **Cal AI revenue (sources conflict by date).** $35M annual run rate reported in May 2025; more than 15 million downloads and over $30M annual revenue in under two years per an agency case study; another 2026 source reports about $30M for 2025 and a $5.7M month in January 2026 (carrying page not confirmed; candidates are the two 2026 links below); FunnelFox's title calls it a "$50M growth engine". — [Latka, May 2025](https://getlatka.com/interviews/cal-ai-zach-yadegari-2025); [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/cal-ai-brand-influencer-marketing-strategy/); [RevenueCat Sub Club with Jake Castillo, 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/jake-castillo-cal-ai-sub-club-podcast-2026); [Stormy AI playbook 2026](https://stormy.ai/blog/cal-ai-tiktok-marketing-playbook-2026); [FunnelFox blog](https://blog.funnelfox.com/cal-ai-influencer-marketing/)
- **Ceiling of creator-only growth.** One write-up says influencer marketing took Cal AI to about $2M/month, after which performance ads on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook were needed to grow further. — [Founder Playbooks](https://founder-playbooks.pages.dev/insight/influencer-to-paid-ads-growth-ladder)
- **Owned accounts.** In January 2025 Cal AI reportedly launched more than a dozen branded TikTok accounts as a parallel distribution system. — [8x.social](https://www.8x.social/en/blog/cal-ai-multi-account)
- **Platform enforcement risk hit the same app.** Adapty reports Apple briefly removed Cal AI from the App Store in April 2026 over web payment violations. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/app-to-web-paywalls-ios-one-year-data/)
- **Quittr.** Founder Alex Slater (19, London, now San Francisco); growth attributed mainly to influencer marketing plus organic social; Starter Story's breakdown is titled about $50K/month, and a vc.ru piece relays a claim of about $35K revenue in a single day. Tactics and CPMs were not in the summaries. — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/quittr-breakdown); [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/growth/1762617-kak-quittr-dorosli-do-35k-den)

- **TikTok creator tiers (2026 rate cards; per-guide attribution not verified).** Nano (under 10K followers) $50-200 per video; micro (10K-100K) $200-800; macro (100K-1M) $800-5,000. Some guides put micro at $100-500. — [Sideshift](https://sideshift.app/blog/ugc-creator-rates-by-platform); [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/micro-influencer-rates/)
- **Creator CPM benchmarks (2026; per-guide attribution not verified).** TikTok $3-15, Instagram about $5-15, YouTube $10-30; paid in-feed ads $5-30 CPM versus $1.62 organic creator CPM in one 2025 campaign (single case, not a benchmark). — [Sideshift, influencer campaign costs](https://sideshift.app/blog/influencer-campaigns-cost); [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/cost-of-creator-partnerships/)

- **Account farms: vendor claims only.** A vendor promoting physical "phone farms" claims 15 accounts can generate over 4.7 million views in under a month, with no methodology; vendors disagree on what a "warmed" account is (about 500+ versus about 1,000 views per post). — [Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/blog/how-to-scale-tiktok-accounts-shadowban-fix); [Schedpilot](https://schedpilot.com/the-complete-guide-to-tiktok-account-warming/)

Guides warn that stock photos, recycled screenshots, and new accounts that post branded content immediately get suppressed. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/11-tiktok-videos-6-weeks-exactly-0-views-it-wasnt-a-shadowban-63e5b31f96); [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-account-warm-up/)

### Inferences
- A workable small-budget version of the Cal AI model is 5-20 nano/micro creators on small retainers judged on effective CPM (fee divided by delivered views times 1,000), aiming at or below roughly $5; at $175 per video a creator needs about 35,000 views per video to hit that.
- Creator seeding is reported to plateau; teams that scaled past it moved the winning creator videos into paid ads. This implies creator content doubles as the creative-testing pipeline for paid UA.
- Account farms and faceless slideshow accounts are the weakest-evidenced tactic in this section: the only performance numbers are from vendors selling the tooling, simulated-organic multi-account operation conflicts with platform rules, and the one first-hand report retrieved is a failure. Flag as declining/risky.
- TikTok's US ban risk has receded since January 2026, but two concentration risks remain: algorithm retraining under the new entity, and Apple's willingness to pull even a top-grossing app (Cal AI, April 2026) for payment-rule violations.

## 4. Paid UA on Meta, TikTok, Google and AppLovin for subscription apps

### Takeaway
Reported US iOS install costs for subscription apps sit around $2-5.50, which at median funnel rates implies roughly $250 per paying subscriber; early return on ad spend is low by design (15-30% at day 7) and payback is typically 4-5 months, which is why paid-UA-led apps lean on weekly plans and short trials that bring cash forward. No trustworthy cost-per-trial table by category and network was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **CPI ranges (2026 vendor blogs; per-source attribution not confirmed).** US iOS about $2.00-5.50 per install (Android about $1.00-3.00); another source gives $1.50-5.00 for US iOS and $0.50-2.00 for US Android; Fitness $2-8 on Meta and TikTok. — [SEM Nexus](https://semnexus.com/cpi-benchmarks-app-category-platform-2026/); [The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/mobile-app-cpi-benchmarks-2026)
- **Funnel math (2026 vendor explainer; exact page not confirmed).** With a median trial start rate of 6.2% and median trial-to-paid of 34.8%, a $5 CPI implies about $250 per paying user. — [The Social Outline, break-even CPI](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/break-even-cpi-subscription-app); [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/subscription-app-ua-budget)
- **Best dataset identified but not opened.** Adapty's Meta Ads benchmark report (Q3 2026) covers CPI, cost per trial and cost per paying user by platform and category from about 5,000 subscription apps in 45 countries. — [Adapty Meta Ads benchmarks](https://adapty.io/meta-ads-benchmarks/)
- **ROAS curve (agency benchmark table, 2026).** Subscription apps: day-7 ROAS 15-30%, day-30 40-70%, day-90 70-110%. — [Admiral Media benchmarks 2026](https://admiral.media/mobile-app-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)
- **Payback.** Adapty's 2026 report, as relayed by Admiral Media, says consumer apps typically recover acquisition cost within 4-5 months when acquisition is well optimized. — [Admiral Media, CAC payback](https://admiral.media/cac-payback-period-mobile-apps/)
- **Why day-0 numbers mislead.** 90% of trials start on day 0 but only 44.5% of paid conversions do. — [Admiral Media, retention-first UA](https://admiral.media/retention-first-user-acquisition/)
- **Weekly plans and paid UA.** Weekly plans are reported at about 55% of app subscription revenue (55.5%, up from 43.3% in 2023). The search summaries attribute this once to Adapty and once to RevenueCat; RevenueCat's own 2026 report is summarized as saying weekly plans rarely exceed 30% of category revenue outside Gaming. — [Adapty key findings 2026](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/); [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/retention-first-user-acquisition/); contradicted by [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- **Weekly churn.** Business of Apps data, relayed by Admiral Media: over 98% of weekly subscriptions churn before one year, versus 92% of monthly and 73% of annual. — [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/retention-first-user-acquisition/)
- **Weekly LTV penalty.** A channel bringing weekly subscribers to a Health & Fitness app yields roughly one third of the lifetime value of a channel bringing annual subscribers at the same CPI (vendor blog; carrying page not confirmed). — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/tips/blog/mobile-app-acquisition-roi-beyond-cpi)
- **Offer shapes convert differently.** Adapty notes a $2.99 weekly plan with free trial converts very differently from a $79.99 annual plan behind a hard paywall, so benchmarks must match offer type. — [Adapty, profitable ads](https://adapty.io/blog/profitable-ads-for-subscription-apps/)
- **Meta attribution change (unconfirmed).** Adapty reports that Meta's 2026 removal of 7-day-view and 28-day-view attribution windows widened the gap between reported and real ROAS; not checked against Meta. — [Adapty, Meta ads Q&A](https://adapty.io/blog/meta-ads-subscription-apps/)
- **Attribution frameworks.** AdAttributionKit is Apple's current framework and successor to SKAdNetwork, interoperable with it; it gained multiple conversion tracking, custom attribution rules and country codes on 11 June 2025 and received no update at WWDC 2026. A single vendor blog says SKAdNetwork endpoints are deprecated in late 2026; unconfirmed. — [Singular](https://www.singular.net/ko/blog/apple-search-ads-skadnetwork-adattributionkit/); [Linkrunner](https://deeplinks.linkrunner.io/blog/adattributionkit-vs-skan)
- **TikTok measurement.** TikTok and Kochava launched real-time iOS conversion reporting on 21 October 2025 to work around SKAdNetwork delays (carrying page not confirmed). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/mobile-measurement-partner/)
- **MMP costs (third-party claims, not vendor pricing pages).** AppsFlyer's free "Zero" plan is described as covering up to 10,000 conversions per month, with paid tiers about $0.05-0.12 per conversion; Adjust is custom-priced and "typically starts at $1,000/month"; enterprise MMPs run $1K-20K+/month. — [Sonar, do you need an MMP](https://trysonar.app/blog/mobile-measurement-partner-do-you-need-one); [Airbridge MMP comparison](https://www.airbridge.io/blog/appsflyer-vs-airbridge-vs-adjust-vs-branch-best-mmp-for-subscription-app-in-2026)

### Inferences
- At $250 per paying user, an app needs well over $250 of net revenue per subscriber to scale US paid UA; with weekly churn above 98% inside a year, weekly plans work for paid UA because they return cash in days, not because they hold users. This is a cash-flow tactic with thin margin for error.
- A small team should not judge campaigns on day-0 or day-7 ROAS alone: fewer than half of eventual paid conversions happen on day 0, and day-7 ROAS of 15-30% is normal for campaigns that later break even.
- Below roughly a few thousand dollars a month of spend an MMP is hard to justify; free tiers plus the subscription platform's own attribution integrations are the usual substitute (inference from the price points above, not a sourced claim).
- The Adapty-versus-RevenueCat disagreement on weekly share most likely reflects different customer bases (paid-UA-heavy apps versus a broader indie mix), not a factual contradiction.

## 5. Web-to-app funnels and web checkout

### Takeaway
In the US, Apple currently collects no commission on purchases made through external links, but the rate is unsettled (the district court is to decide what "reasonable" commission, if any, Apple may charge), and tests show web checkout converts worse than in-app purchase inside the app, so net take-home was roughly flat in RevenueCat's own experiment. Web quiz funnels are a separate play (acquire on the web, skip the store) with vendor-reported higher lifetime value but no independent refund or chargeback data.

### Cited Findings
- **US legal state.** After the April 2025 contempt order, zero commission on qualifying external purchases is the rule in force; the Ninth Circuit upheld the contempt finding but allowed a "reasonable" commission to be considered; in late April 2026 it reversed a stay that had let Apple keep its commission structure while seeking Supreme Court review; the case returned to Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers to set what Apple may collect on linked purchases, if anything. — [MacRumors, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/29/epic-games-wins-reversal-app-store-fee-battle/)
- **Supreme Court.** Apple filed an application to stay the mandate pending certiorari (No. 25A1213), logged 5 May 2026; no outcome was found. — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/apple-epic-supreme-court-2026)
- **Mid-2026 practice.** Unity's July 2026 note says no platform commission is currently collected on US link-out purchases. — [Unity](https://unity.com/blog/mobile-game-webshop-business-case)
- **Scope.** RevenueCat's guidance: in the US, external payment links are allowed in all App Store categories; in-app purchase still carries the standard 15-30%. From 20 June 2026, iOS apps in Brazil can offer alternative in-app payments and link out, with Apple's in-app purchase presented alongside. — [RevenueCat, anti-steering strategy](https://revenuecat.com/blog/growth/apple-anti-steering-ruling-monetization-strategy/)
- **EU and UK.** Apple moved to a modular fee model in the EU on 1 January 2026; UK developers still pay the standard 15-30%. The European Commission fined Apple EUR 500 million for breaching the DMA anti-steering rules. — [Foresight Mobile](https://foresightmobile.com/blog/app-store-fees-2026-web-to-app-billing); [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/web-to-app-funnels-subscription-apps/)
- **Fee arithmetic (vendor model).** On a $79.99 annual plan, Stripe processing costs about $2.62 versus $24.00 at Apple's 30% or $12.00 at 15%; this ignores taxes, refunds and disputes. — [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/?p=5518)
- **RevenueCat's controlled test (Dipsea app, US iOS, May 2025).** Initial conversion: in-app purchase only 27.0%; in-app plus web 23.5%; web only 18.1%. Net per user about $1.96 for web-only versus about $2.03 for in-app after Apple's 30%; RevenueCat's headline is a 6% drop in take-home for web. Trial-to-paid was 26.3% on web versus 25.0% in-app. When both were offered, 132 users chose in-app and 68 chose web despite a web discount. — [RevenueCat test](https://revenuecat.com/blog/growth/iap-vs-web-purchases-conversion-test); [Daring Fireball, 15 May 2025](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/05/15/revenuecat-external-purchase-report); [Michael Tsai, 14 May 2025](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2025/05/14/external-purchase-conversion-metrics)
- **Contrasting data (Adapty, one year in, 2026).** Across other apps, purchase per paywall view was 3.93% on web versus 2.54% native, but revenue per paying user was $52.83 on web versus $65.99 native. Adapty also notes the commission rate is still being negotiated. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/app-to-web-paywalls-ios-one-year-data/)
- **Enforcement risk.** Apple briefly removed Cal AI from the App Store in April 2026 for web payment violations. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/app-to-web-paywalls-ios-one-year-data/)
- **Vendor caution.** RevenueCat has published a piece titled "Why you shouldn't use app-to-web" (headline only). — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/why-you-shouldnt-use-app-to-web)
- **Web quiz funnels (Adapty-based index of 250 web quiz funnel apps, 2026).** Web-to-app funnel conversion 1.10% versus 1.60% for iOS in-app; median paid-trial price $4.99; median renewal $41.99 per month; no free trials in the sample. — [Consumer App Index](https://cai-breakdowns.pages.dev/web2app-paywalls-2026)
- **FunnelFox report (via a June 2026 podcast summary).** Full-funnel conversion about 2x higher on web than in-app, attributed largely to skipping the store install step; web lifetime value about 2x in-app on annual plans and at least 50% higher on monthly; an easy refund path plus a save offer is said to lower chargebacks (no rate given). — [Podcast episode, 25 Jun 2026](https://podcastaddict.com/episode/https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.transistor.fm%2F24f777a0%2Fa72f58f5.mp3&podcastId=6124718); conflicts with the 1.10% versus 1.60% figure in [Consumer App Index](https://cai-breakdowns.pages.dev/web2app-paywalls-2026)

### Inferences
- For an app under Apple's 15% small-business rate, in-app web checkout is unlikely to pay off: RevenueCat's test showed roughly break-even against a 30% commission, so against 15% the conversion loss would outweigh the fee saving. The saving case is strongest for apps paying 30% and for high-priced annual plans.
- The two "web" plays should not be conflated. An in-app link to web checkout competes with Apple's one-tap purchase and loses conversion; a web quiz funnel changes where the user is acquired and lets Meta/TikTok optimize on real purchase events, which is where the lifetime-value claims come from.
- Web quiz funnel pricing in the sample (paid $4.99 trials, about $42/month renewals) is far more aggressive than typical in-app pricing; this is the pattern regulators target (see section 6), and chargeback exposure moves from Apple to the developer.
- Legal volatility is the main planning risk: a "reasonable commission" could be set at any time, so web checkout economics should be modelled with a non-zero Apple fee as a downside case.

## 6. Paywall and pricing tactics, testing tools, and what Apple and regulators reject

### Takeaway
The data favours showing a paywall early and firmly: hard paywalls convert about five times more downloads to paid than freemium with no retention penalty on annual plans, and most trial cancellations happen in the first session. Apple began rejecting "free trial toggle" paywalls in January 2026, and US regulators keep enforcing against hard-to-cancel subscriptions even though the federal click-to-cancel rule was struck down.

### Cited Findings
- **Dataset.** RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 covers over 115,000 apps, $16B in revenue and more than a billion transactions. — [RevenueCat report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [SaaStr](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth)
- **Hard versus soft paywall (RevenueCat 2026).** Median conversion to paid by day 35 is 10.7% for hard-paywall apps versus 2.1% for freemium. The search summary labelled this "trial-to-paid"; RevenueCat's prior-year equivalent is download-to-paid, so the denominator needs checking. One-year retention on annual plans is nearly identical: 28% freemium versus 27% hard paywall. — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026); [SaaStr](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth)
- **Trial length (RevenueCat 2026).** Trials of 17-32 days have a median trial-to-paid of 42.5% versus 25.5% for trials of 4 days or less; yet the share of apps using trials of 4 days or less rose from 42.1% (2025) to 46.5% (2026). — [PricePush summary](https://pricepush.app/blog/trial-to-paid-conversion-rate-benchmarks); [RevenueCat report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- **First-session cancellations.** 55% of all 3-day-trial cancellations happen on day 0. — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- **Short trials dominate (Adapty State of Paywalls 2026, 78,000+ paywalls).** 79% of trials last 1-3 days; the median US app converts 2.75% of paywall viewers versus 1.38% for the median European app. — [Adapty, paywall tests](https://adapty.io/blog/paywall-optimization-tests/)
- **Price and retention.** Lower-priced apps show 36% median retention versus 23% for higher-priced apps; higher-priced apps convert downloads to trials at nearly 2x the rate of low-priced ones (8.9% for the higher-priced group; comparison figure truncated; carrying pages not confirmed). — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026); [Data Analysis Journal](https://dataanalysis.substack.com/p/2026-benchmarks-more-apps-less-quality)
- **Plan mix by app type (RevenueCat 2026).** Non-AI apps skew more to yearly (41.8% versus 24% for AI apps) and weekly (30.3% versus 15%) durations. — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- **Trials do not always raise value (Adapty 2026, about $3B revenue, 16,000+ apps).** Productivity has the highest average lifetime value at $46.97; within Productivity, direct buyers are worth $56.95 versus $49.13 for trial users; trials lowered lifetime value in 4 of 8 categories. — [Adapty, Productivity benchmarks](https://adapty.io/blog/productivity-app-subscription-benchmarks/); [Adapty report](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/)
- **Discounting.** In Health & Fitness, 90% of subscriptions sell at full price. — [Adapty, Health & Fitness benchmarks](https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/)
- **Price localization (Adapty 2026).** Subscription prices vary 4x between countries; UK, France, Germany, Italy and Spain index at about 1.2x the US price, India at 0.6x and Turkey at 0.7x. — [Adapty key findings 2026](https://adapty.io/blog/mobile-app-monetization-2026/)
- **Win-back is weak for annual plans.** Only about 5% of subscribers who cancel an annual plan come back, per coverage of RevenueCat's 2026 report. — [9to5Mac, 27 May 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/27/new-report-shows-annual-app-subscribers-rarely-return-after-they-cancel/)
- **Apple rejects trial toggles (since January 2026).** Developers report rejections under Guideline 3.1.2 for paywalls with a switch that adds or removes a free trial, with no announcement or grace period; Apple's notice calls out the toggle specifically. — [RevenueCat, "R.I.P. toggle paywall"](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/rip-toggle-paywall); [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/your-toggle-paywall-is-about-to-get-rejected/)
- **Apple's reasoning (second-hand).** Superwall, citing an off-the-record conversation with Apple, says toggles were abused to silently switch the selected product (for example monthly to annual) when the "trial" was enabled; Apple wants the trial attached to a clearly described product. The same post covers Apple's position on external checkout and A/B testing. — [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/external-checkout-a-b-testing-and-trial-toggles-confirmed-apples-rules-for-ios)
- **Compliant replacement.** Offer the trial as its own clearly labelled plan; Flo replaced its "Enable free trial" toggle with a specific 14-day-trial plan option. — [RevenueCat, "R.I.P. toggle paywall"](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/rip-toggle-paywall)
- **Related rejections.** A 2026 Apple developer forum thread reports a 3.1.2(c) rejection where "Try for Free" was judged too conspicuous relative to the billed price. — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/841626)
- **FTC click-to-cancel.** The Eighth Circuit vacated the 2024 rule in full on 8 July 2025 on procedural grounds. The FTC restarted rulemaking with an advance notice of proposed rulemaking in early 2026 (reported as March 2026); a final rule is likely more than a year away. — [Gibson Dunn, Feb 2026](https://www.gibsondunn.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues.pdf); [Jones Day, May 2026](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/05/ftc-revives-clicktocancel-rule-new-risks-for-subscription-businesses); [Sidley, Feb 2026](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2026/02/us-ftc-signals-renewed-interest-in-click-to-cancel-rulemaking)
- **Enforcement continues without the rule.** The FTC keeps suing under ROSCA and Section 5 (for example the August 2025 case against the operators of LA Fitness); state auto-renewal laws carry similar requirements; New York City's click-to-cancel rule takes effect 1 October 2026. — [Winston & Strawn](https://www.winston.com/en/blogs-and-podcasts/direct-sellers-update-regulation-law-and-policy/no-more-click-to-cancelrule-but-ftcs-enforcement-actions-keep-coming); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/1760520/ftc-reopens-the-click-to-cancel-debate-new-negative-option-anprm-puts-subscription-businesses-on-notice)
- **Testing-platform pricing (2026 third-party comparisons; verify on vendor pages).** RevenueCat: free up to $2,500 monthly tracked revenue, then about 1% (one comparison lists a $49/month plus 1% tier and a $199/month tier). Superwall: free up to $10K monthly attributed revenue, then usage-based; its page and FAQ reportedly differ on the 1% fee (as of 4 Nov 2026). Adapty: free tier $5K monthly tracked revenue per its 2026 pricing (older listings say $10K), paid tiers $99-499/month. — [BuildMVPFast comparison](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/revenuecat-vs-superwall-vs-adapty-in-app-subscription-indie-2026); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/adapty-vs-revenuecat); [PricePush, 4 Nov 2026](https://pricepush.app/blog/superwall-vs-revenuecat-numbers)

### Inferences
- The default for a new small app is a hard or near-hard paywall at the end of onboarding: about 5x the paid conversion of freemium with the same annual retention. Freemium is only justified when free users create value (virality, content, network effects).
- Since more than half of 3-day-trial cancellations happen in the first session, the first session, not the reminder flow, decides trial conversion; this supports longer personalized onboarding before the paywall, though no direct onboarding-length data was retrieved.
- The market is moving to shorter trials even though longer trials convert better per trial; the likely reason is cash-flow speed for paid UA (section 4). A small team not dependent on paid UA can test 7-day or longer trials against the trend.
- Purchasing-power pricing is cheap to implement and clearly practised (0.6-0.7x in India and Turkey, 1.2x in Western Europe); combined with the Apple Ads geo data in section 2, cheaper markets can be profitable only if prices are localized rather than left at auto-converted US levels.
- Compliance line as of 2026: no trial toggles; billed amount more prominent than "free"; cancellation as easy as sign-up for anything sold on the web. Web funnels lose Apple's built-in cancellation flow, so the developer inherits the ROSCA and state-law exposure directly.

Not usable as general benchmarks without reading the report. — [Adapty report](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/)

## 7. Retention and re-engagement levers

### Takeaway
Very little lever-level evidence was retrieved. What the data does show is that retention is decided early (first-session cancellations) and that lapsed annual subscribers rarely return (about 5%), so prevention matters more than win-back; AI apps monetize better per payer but retain markedly worse.

### Cited Findings
- **Renewal curve (Adapty 2026).** Share of subscribers remaining: 45.1% at first renewal, 37.1% at second, 31.6% at third, 27.6% at fourth, 8.3% at fifth. The fifth-renewal drop looks anomalous in the summary and needs checking against the report. — [Adapty report](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/)
- **Annual retention (RevenueCat 2026).** About 27-28% of annual subscribers remain after one year regardless of paywall type. — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- **AI apps (RevenueCat 2026).** 12-month retention on monthly plans is 6.1% for AI apps versus 9.5% for non-AI; annual-plan retention 21.1% versus 30.7%; AI apps earn 41% more revenue per payer over one year. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- **Win-back ceiling.** Only about 5% of annual-plan cancellers come back. — [9to5Mac, 27 May 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/27/new-report-shows-annual-app-subscribers-rarely-return-after-they-cancel/)
- **Usage retention medians (MWM, US, Q3 2025, apps with at least 1,000 day-30 downloads, platforms blended), day 1 / day 7 / day 30.** Social & Communication 31.9% / 12.3% / 5.9%; Lifestyle & Well-being 23.6% / 9.6% / 4.8%; Productivity & Tools 23.0% / 8.9% / 4.5%; Education & Knowledge 24.9% / 8.6% / 3.6%. — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/glossary/retention)
- **Platform split (Adjust data via a secondary roundup).** iOS day-1 retention 27% versus 24% on Android; day 30 8% versus 6%. — [Lovable guide](https://lovable.dev/guides/what-is-a-good-retention-rate-for-an-app)
- **Unused, stagnant apps are now a removal risk.** Apple's June 2026 guideline revision says apps in oversaturated categories that are not updated, improved or do not attract customers may be removed. — [MacRumors, 9 Jun 2026](https://macrumors.com/2026/06/09/app-store-guidelines-low-quality-apps/)

### Inferences
- With day-30 usage retention near 4-6% for typical consumer categories, most revenue must be captured in the first sessions; re-engagement features (widgets, Live Activities, streaks, notifications) matter mainly for the minority who convert, to protect renewals.
- Win-back offers for annual cancellers have a low ceiling (about 5% return), so effort is better spent on pre-renewal engagement and cancellation-flow save offers than on post-churn campaigns.
- AI features raise revenue per payer but shorten subscriber life; an AI app should price for a short lifetime (higher price, annual push) rather than assume standard retention.

## 9. Legal and compliance essentials for small publishers in 2026

### Takeaway
The live US age-assurance obligation is Texas, whose App Store Accountability Act has been in effect since a Fifth Circuit stay on 28 May 2026 (appeal pending); Utah and Louisiana were both postponed to mid-2027. Apple's new age-rating tiers and questionnaire already apply to every app, and subscription-cancellation enforcement continues without a federal click-to-cancel rule.

### Cited Findings
- **Texas SB 2420.** A federal judge in Austin preliminarily enjoined the law on 23 December 2025 (likely First Amendment violation, vagueness) before its 1 January 2026 effective date. — [AppleInsider, 23 Dec 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/12/23/texas-judge-says-apple-doesnt-have-to-check-app-store-ages)

### Inferences
- For a small publisher the practical 2026 checklist from retrieved evidence is: complete the new age-rating questionnaire accurately; integrate Apple's age-range signals if the app could be used by minors and is distributed in Texas; treat material feature or monetization changes as "significant changes" that must be flagged; keep cancellation simple wherever billing is off-platform.

## 10. Benchmarks: what "good" looks like for a small app

### Takeaway
The median outcome is poor: the median subscription app earns about $492 per month and most new apps never reach $1,000 in total, while growth concentrates in the top quartile. "Good" for a small app is therefore a hard-paywall-style 10%+ download-to-paid by day 35 (versus about 2% freemium), trial-to-paid of 35-45%, day-30 usage retention above about 5%, and acquisition cost recovered within 4-5 months.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue distribution (RevenueCat 2026).** The median subscription app earns $492 per month, down 22% year on year; the top 25% of apps grew monthly recurring revenue by 80% or more while the bottom 25% shrank by more than 33%. Exact carrying page for the $492 figure not confirmed. — [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/revenuecat-data-shows-subscription-app-growth-concentrating-at-the-top); [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- **Survival.** 57.7% of new apps never reach $1,000 in total revenue (framing not confirmed against the report; carrying page not confirmed). — [Data Analysis Journal](https://dataanalysis.substack.com/p/2026-benchmarks-more-apps-less-quality)
- **Concentration (headline only).** "95% of app subscription revenue goes to top 10%" per Adapty's 2026 report. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/95-of-app-subscription-revenue-goes-to-top-10-adaptys-2026-benchmark-report/)
- **Download-to-trial.** Median trial start rate 6.2% (vendor explainer; see section 4); higher-priced apps reach 8.9%. — [The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/break-even-cpi-subscription-app); [Data Analysis Journal](https://dataanalysis.substack.com/p/2026-benchmarks-more-apps-less-quality)
- **Trial-to-paid.** Median 25.5% for trials of 4 days or less and 42.5% for 17-32 days (RevenueCat 2026); 34.8% cited as an overall median; RevenueCat's 2024 report median was 37.3%. — [PricePush summary](https://pricepush.app/blog/trial-to-paid-conversion-rate-benchmarks); [The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/break-even-cpi-subscription-app)
- **Download-to-paid by day 35.** 10.7% hard paywall versus 2.1% freemium (denominator caveat in section 6). — [SaaStr](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth)
- **Paywall view to purchase.** Median 2.75% in the US, 1.38% in Europe (Adapty State of Paywalls 2026). — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/paywall-optimization-tests/)
- **Usage retention.** Day 1 / day 7 / day 30 medians of roughly 23-32% / 9-12% / 3.6-5.9% by category (MWM, US, Q3 2025). — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/glossary/retention)
- **Subscriber retention.** 27-28% of annual subscribers remain after a year; AI apps 21.1% (annual) and 6.1% (monthly at 12 months); over 98% of weekly subscriptions churn within a year. — [RevenueCat 2026 summary](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/); [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/retention-first-user-acquisition/)
- **Cost per install.** US iOS about $2.00-5.50 on paid social; Apple Ads cost per acquisition from about $1 (Photo & Video) to $14-20 (Sports), about $3.20 at the September peak across categories (2025 data). — [SEM Nexus](https://semnexus.com/cpi-benchmarks-app-category-platform-2026/); [Mobile Action](https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-ads-2026-benchmark-report/executive-summary/); [SplitMetrics](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/executive-summary)
- **Payback and ROAS.** Acquisition cost recovered in 4-5 months when well optimized; day-7 ROAS 15-30%, day-30 40-70%, day-90 70-110%. — [Admiral Media, CAC payback](https://admiral.media/cac-payback-period-mobile-apps/); [Admiral Media benchmarks 2026](https://admiral.media/mobile-app-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)
- **Lifetime value reference.** Productivity leads categories at $46.97 average lifetime value per subscriber (Adapty 2026). — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/productivity-app-subscription-benchmarks/)

### Inferences
- Worked example from the cited medians: 1,000 US installs at $3.50 cost $3,500; at 6.2% trial start and 34.8% trial-to-paid that is about 22 payers, or about $160 per payer. Against a lifetime value near $47 (Adapty's best category average) this loses money; paid UA only works for a small app whose funnel is several times the median (for example hard-paywall 10.7% download-to-paid, which gives about 107 payers and about $33 per payer) or whose price and retention are well above average.
- Hence the realistic sequence for a 1-3 person studio: prove an above-median funnel on free traffic first (ASO, creators, founder content), then add Apple Ads on exact-match terms, then paid social once payer cost sits below first-year net revenue per payer.
- Realistic trajectory: the base rate is failure to reach $1,000 total; headline founder cases ($10K-600K per year) are survivorship examples promoted by tool vendors and should be read as the top decile, not the plan.
