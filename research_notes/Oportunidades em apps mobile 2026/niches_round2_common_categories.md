# Round 2: common consumer App Store categories left without data, plus demand-side evidence (state as of November 2026)

Method note (read before using any number): 35 web searches were run (the hard limit) and 2 page fetches attempted. One fetch worked (developer.apple.com, App Store Awards 2025); the other failed (trysonar.app is outside the session's allowed network domains). Every other figure below comes from search-result summaries, not from reading the page, so wording and scope could not be checked against the original. Labels: **[SR]** = self-reported by the founder or company; **[3P]** = third-party modeled estimate; **[Press]** = press report of a company statement. "(attribution inferred)" means the search summary gave the number without naming the page, and the linked page is the most likely origin. AppGoblin's revenue bands ("<$10K", "$10K+") are far below every other estimator for the same apps (it puts Headway, Quittr and CHANI under $10K a month), so they are shown only to document the disagreement; its install and active-user counts are more useful.

## 1. Habit trackers and to-do/planner apps

### Takeaway
The category has a proven solo-developer ceiling of roughly $28K MRR (HabitKit, end of 2025, self-reported) and produced one breakout new entrant in 2025 (Focus Friend, 1.4M downloads in its first week), while venture-scale leaders such as Finch and Tiimo sit around $250K to $1M a month on third-party estimates. No usable 2025-2026 revenue was found for Streaks, Habitify, Structured or Things.

### Cited Findings
Indie cases
- HabitKit (solo developer Sebastian Röhl): about $28,000 MRR, about $602,000 total revenue and 25,100 active subscribers at the end of 2025 [SR] — [Röhl's 2025 recap](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/2025-the-year-that-changed-everything)
- HabitKit one year earlier: "over $15,000 in MRR" at the end of 2024 [SR] — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/habit-kit-breakdown); the founder credits building in public — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/how-building-in-public-got-my-habit-tracking-mobile-app-to-15k-mo-DCOYyF9O14dBnuGkkaQR)
- HabitKit pricing is described as a subscription of about $2 a month on iOS and $1 on Android (undated) — [Starter Story monetization page](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/habitkit/monetization)
- Conflicting lower figures for HabitKit: a TrustMRR listing shows $6.4K MRR and $156K total, last updated 11 Apr 2026, but the snippet mixed several startups — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/habitkit); AppGoblin estimates 680.6K Android installs and under $10K a month [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.roehl.habitkit)
- Habit Pixel (different solo developer): launched May 2025, reached $1K MRR in January 2026 after eight months, and plans more languages to reach $5K MRR by mid-2026 [SR] — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-0-to-1k-mrr-in-8-months-bootstrapping-habit-pixel-as-a-solo-dev-684b6c056d)
- Supply signal: the same search returned at least five more habit-tracker listings on one revenue directory (HabitKeep, Habit Mosaic, habit grove, Habits Garden, Habstick); no revenue was retrieved for them — [TrustMRR example](https://trustmrr.com/startup/habitkeep)

Leaders and notable entrants
- Finch (self-care pet): about $1M estimated monthly revenue and 600,000+ monthly downloads, undated [3P] — [Adapty paywall library](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/finch/); 2.34M downloads in a 90-day window with 61.78% from the US, undated [3P] — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/kr/blogs/finch-as-app-store-editors-choice-a-self-care-companion/); an AI-generated audit labels its trend "declining" as of April 2026 (low confidence) — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/finch-self-care-pet)
- Focus Friend (gamified focus timer): 1.4M downloads across both stores in its first week; the same article gives 1.9M cumulative devices and $800K net revenue, and separately $370K US net revenue with 83% from the App Store (scopes unclear); it fell to #48 in the US Top Overall chart within two weeks (September 2025) [3P] — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250905/1-the-app-that-dethroned-chatgpt-isnt-what-youd-expect)
- Focus Friend won a 2025 App Store Award for Cultural Impact; Tiimo won iPhone App of the Year; Structured was an iPad App of the Year finalist (announced 4 Dec 2025) — [Apple developer site](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-store-awards-2025/)
- Tiimo: about $250K estimated monthly revenue and 65K monthly installs, undated [3P] — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/discover/apps/tiimo-ai-plan-focus-to-do/)
- Todoist: company-level revenue band of $15M to $25M a year as of March 2026 [3P] — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/company/todoist.com); AppGoblin's "$10K+ a month" for the iOS listing is about two orders of magnitude lower [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/572688855)
- Things 3 is a paid download at $9.99 on iPhone with no in-app purchases — [App Pricing Lab](https://www.apppricinglab.com/app/apple/904237743)

### Inferences
- HabitKit's self-reported MRR went from about $15K to about $28K during 2025, so a well-positioned indie was still growing in a crowded category; the long tail (Habit Pixel at $1K MRR after eight months, many listings with no visible revenue) shows the typical outcome is far smaller.
- The entry angles with evidence are distribution (building in public for HabitKit), a gamified companion (Finch, Focus Friend) and positioning for people who find planning stressful (Tiimo, which Apple praised for turning "stressful tasks into calm ones"). Feature parity alone has no evidence of working.
- Focus Friend's fall from #1 to #48 in two weeks suggests launch spikes in this category do not hold without retention mechanics.

### Gaps
- No revenue or download estimates were found for Streaks, Habitify, Structured, Things 3 (beyond price), TickTick or Productive. Appfigures profile pages exist but returned no numbers in search.
- Finch figures are undated; no Sensor Tower or Appfigures number was found.
- Unverified background knowledge (not from this session): Focus Friend was launched by the creator Hank Green, which would explain the launch spike; HabitKit's hallmark is a GitHub-style contribution grid.

## 2. Quit-addiction, looksmaxxing/face-rating, and dating helpers ("rizz")

### Takeaway
Trend-driven apps in this group peaked fast and fell: Umax dropped to about $173K a month by August 2024 and the "rizz" search trend has been falling since March 2024, yet successors still show roughly $95K a month on a founder revenue directory in 2026. Quittr has no trustworthy 2026 figure; published estimates range from under $10K a month to a claimed $35K a day.

### Cited Findings
Quit-addiction and sobriety
- Quittr: the founder-reported figure was $50K a month in December 2024 [SR] — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/quittr-breakdown); an undated listing also shows $50K estimated monthly revenue [3P] — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/quittr-quit-porn-now/); a Russian-language post is titled "How QUITTR grew to $35K a day", uncorroborated — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/growth/1762617-kak-quittr-dorosli-do-35k-den); AppGoblin's 2026 data shows 124K monthly active users on iOS and 30.3K on Android with revenue "under $10K" [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6532588521)
- The publisher has a second listing, "Quit Smoking - Quittr", indicating a brand extension to other habits — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6503163176)
- Reframe (alcohol reduction): the company claimed 3.2M downloads, that 91% of users cut drinking substantially within three months and that 40% stay on the paid program beyond a year (January 2025 press) [SR] — [Hypepotamus](https://hypepotamus.com/startup-news/atlanta-reframe-app-dry-january-success-data-2026/) (attribution inferred); it reported over $10M ARR in 2022 [SR] — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/03/05/reframe-app-moderate-drinking-raises-venture-capital); a current estimate is $250K a month and 50K installs a month, undated [3P] — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/discover/apps/reframe-drink-less-thrive/) (attribution inferred)
- A 2026 review says Reframe had a data breach in May 2026 and faces three pending class actions (secondary source, not verified) — [Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/reframe-app-review/)
- I Am Sober: 17.8M installs and 738K monthly active users [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/672904239); it is mostly free, with Sober Plus at $39.99 a year — [Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/i-am-sober-vs-reframe/)

Looksmaxxing and face rating
- Umax earned about $2M from its early-2024 launch to August 2024; monthly revenue fell 52% to $173K in August 2024; rival LooksMax fell 28% to $53K; downloads had declined since March 2024 [3P] — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/tiktok-trends-non-gaming-apps)
- Umax's founder claimed about $500,000 a month in mid-2024; the article notes it could not be verified and raises teen mental-health concerns [SR] — [Fortune, 1 Jul 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/07/01/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-faces-mental-health)
- 2026 successors on a founder revenue directory: an AI face-rating app with AI coach at $98K revenue in the last 30 days — [TrustMRR: FaceMax](https://trustmrr.com/startup/facemax-ai-face-rating-plam?metric=mrr); Maxify at $94K in 30 days — [TrustMRR: Maxify](https://trustmrr.com/startup/maxify?metric=mrr); a third, "Looksmaxing App", at $63.96 in 30 days, down 26% — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/looksmaxing-app.md) (all [SR], period 2026, not independently verified)
- Social interest persists: Brandwatch counted over 806,000 mentions of "looksmaxxing" between September 2025 and early 2026 (secondhand) — [Plisio](https://plisio.net/th/ai/looksmax-ai)

Dating helpers
- Plug AI: $200,000 a month, freemium (undated) — [Venture Radar Substack](https://ventureradar.substack.com/p/this-23-year-olds-ai-app-makes-200kmonth); $250,000 a month and 35,000 monthly installs (undated) [3P] — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/plug-ai-texting-assistant)
- RIZZ (TREND IT LLC): 2.2M installs, 94.4K monthly active users, revenue split 43% in-app purchase and 57% ads [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1663430725)
- Clones keep launching and stay tiny: KaRizz AI has about 45 monthly active users [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6636467435); a "Plug AI: Rizz Dating Assistant" look-alike has about 204 [3P] — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6748056633); "Rizz Wing" launched on Android in June 2026 with no ratings — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.rizzwing)
- The decline in face-rating downloads tracked the fall in "Rizz" search interest from March 2024 [3P] — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/tiktok-trends-non-gaming-apps)

Policy context
- No report was found of Apple removing face-rating or looksmaxxing apps. The enforcement found concerns "nudify" apps: in July 2026 San Francisco's city attorney demanded removal of 13 face-swap apps, and Apple removed three and terminated their developers' accounts — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/17/demand-to-pull-nudify-apps/)
- A watchdog counted 47 undressing apps on the App Store with $117M lifetime revenue per AppMagic — [Tech Transparency Project](https://www.techtransparencyproject.org/articles/nudify-apps-widely-available-in-apple-and-google-app-stores)

### Inferences
- These are trend-cycle categories: the first mover captured a few months of high revenue, then lost half its revenue in a month. A new entrant is buying a short window, not a durable business, unless it converts users to a longer program (as Reframe and Quittr try to do).
- The two 2026 face-rating listings near $95K a month suggest the niche did not die after Umax; it fragmented among successors with heavier monetization (the FaceMax listing itself advertises retention offers and a deep-linked paywall).
- Policy risk evidence is indirect: regulators and Apple are acting on AI apps that manipulate faces and bodies, and face rating for teens has drawn mental-health press. Combined with the age-assurance laws noted in round 1, apps aimed at teenage boys carry the highest exposure in this group.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 revenue for Umax, no data for "brain rot" blockers beyond round 1, and nothing on skin or hair analysis apps.
- No reliable 2026 revenue for Quittr, Reframe or I Am Sober; no growth-or-decline series since 2024 for any of them.
- No source on Wing AI specifically.

## 5. Widgets/wallpapers/themes, budgeting and expense trackers, scanner/PDF/fax/translator/QR utilities

### Takeaway
Budgeting is the clearest growth story in this round: Monarch reached $100M ARR in September 2026, up 67% in nine months, after absorbing Mint's users. Widgets are a declining, ad-heavy category except for Locket, which turned a widget into a social network. Utilities are fragmented, with a few listings at $100K+ a month and most under $10K; no paid-acquisition spend data was found.

### Cited Findings
Widgets and themes

- Locket Widget: over 90 million downloads and 9 million daily active users by October 2025 [Press] — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html); more than 100,000 paying subscribers, a 15-person team and profitable since 2024 (3 Nov 2025) [Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/03/lockets-social-app-is-picking-up-steam-with-gen-alpha)

## 7. Where new entrants still succeeded in 2025-2026, and with what angle

### Takeaway
The documented wins in these categories came from distribution and positioning, not from a new feature in an old format: an existing audience (Focus Friend, HabitKit), an incumbent's exit (Monarch after Mint), a new surface turned social (Locket), a trend successor with aggressive monetization (face-rating apps), or AI layered on a trusted tracker (Huckleberry). Evidence that one-time pricing or localization alone wins is absent.

### Cited Findings
- Audience-led launch: Focus Friend reached 1.4M downloads in its first week (reported 5 Sep 2025) and later won an App Store Award, but dropped to #48 within two weeks — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250905/1-the-app-that-dethroned-chatgpt-isnt-what-youd-expect); [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-store-awards-2025/)
- Build-in-public solo developer: HabitKit grew from over $15K MRR (end of 2024) to about $28K MRR (end of 2025) at about $2 a month [SR] — [Röhl's 2025 recap](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/2025-the-year-that-changed-everything); [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/habit-kit-breakdown)
- Slow solo path with localization planned: Habit Pixel, launched May 2025, $1K MRR after eight months [SR] — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-0-to-1k-mrr-in-8-months-bootstrapping-habit-pixel-as-a-solo-dev-684b6c056d)
- Incumbent exit: Monarch's subscribers rose about 20-fold after Mint closed, reaching $100M ARR by September 2026 — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/monarch-money-at-100m-arr/)
- Platform surface turned into a network: Locket, 15 people, profitable, over 100,000 subscribers, growing with Gen Alpha (November 2025) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/03/lockets-social-app-is-picking-up-steam-with-gen-alpha)
- Trend successors: two face-rating apps at $94K to $98K revenue in 30 days in 2026, two years after Umax's decline [SR] — [TrustMRR: FaceMax](https://trustmrr.com/startup/facemax-ai-face-rating-plam?metric=mrr); [TrustMRR: Maxify](https://trustmrr.com/startup/maxify?metric=mrr)
- AI layered on an established tracker: Huckleberry's "Berry" (February 2026) — [OCBJ](https://www.ocbj.com/oc-homepage/berry-provides-specialized-expert-vetted-ai-to-help-with-babies-young-children/)
- Neurodivergent-friendly planning recognized by Apple: Tiimo as iPhone App of the Year 2025 — [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-store-awards-2025/)
- Portfolio and creator-partnership routes appear as founder interview titles only (bodies not read): a 30-app portfolio at $22K a month in under a year, a portfolio at $60K a month, and a creator partnership reaching $50K a month — [Indie Hackers: 30-app portfolio](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/myy3U7K9evxGOVOHti8s); [Indie Hackers: $60K portfolio](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/LD7oNYzKSmWucRfKV1AO); [Indie Hackers: creator partnership](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/partnering-up-with-a-content-creator-to-hit-50k-mo-QZTlkTd3spxX5N2rNc3Z)
- One-time purchase: a vendor guide calls it "largely a niche in 2026" that works for focused utilities at $3.99 to $9.99 and says subscriptions sit behind most $1K to $10K a month solo stories — [The Swift Kit](https://theswiftk.it.com/blog/how-much-do-indie-ios-developers-make); a founder argues a one-time fee is easier for buyers to justify than another subscription — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/why-im-charging-49-once-instead-of-a-subscription-for-my-desktop-app-aabcd91ddb)
- A vendor analysis says most "overnight" indie successes took 12 to 36 months (unsourced generalization) — [Niches Hunter](https://nicheshunter.app/blog/from-idea-to-10k-mrr-what-successful-indie-apps-have-in-common)

### Inferences
- Ranking the round-2 categories by evidence of room for a small team: habit and focus tools (two documented indie-scale wins and Apple editorial support) rank first; manual, buy-once budgeting and recipe organizers are plausible but have no revenue proof; baby tracking, sleep tracking, astrology and book summaries have real money but entrenched leaders; widgets, generic scanners, QR readers, rizz and face-rating apps are either declining, ad-funded or short-lived.
- Across the cases, the common factor is a distribution advantage that existed before or outside the App Store listing. None of the wins found is attributed to keyword discovery alone.
- Low price can work at volume for a solo developer (HabitKit's 25,100 subscribers at about $2 a month), which is the opposite of the $90 to $110 a year price points found at Headway ($89.99), Puppr ($99.99), Monarch ($99.99) and CHANI ($107.99). I Am Sober's $39.99 a year sits between the two models.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 new-entrant success was found in astrology, journaling, sleep, pets, book summaries, scanners or widgets (other than Locket, which predates the window).
- No case was found where localization or one-time pricing was shown, with numbers, to be the reason for success.
- Founder-interview bodies on Indie Hackers and Starter Story could not be opened, so the apps behind the portfolio and creator-partnership headlines are unidentified.
