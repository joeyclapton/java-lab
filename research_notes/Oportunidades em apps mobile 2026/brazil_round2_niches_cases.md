## 1. Top-grossing and top-downloaded apps in Brazil (2025-2026), leading categories, local apps, and BRL prices of global apps

### Takeaway
Store-billed revenue in Brazil is dominated by global platforms: ChatGPT alone was estimated at about US$99M in Brazil in 2025, followed at a distance by Google/YouTube subscriptions, streaming and creator tools, while the download charts are led by social video, AI assistants, government apps and (in 2026) short-drama apps. Global apps price well below US levels in BRL (Duolingo's annual plan was R$179.90 in April 2025; ChatGPT added a R$39.99 tier), and category leaders in Health & Fitness earn only about US$100-135K per week in Brazil.

### Inferences
- On store billing, one global AI app out-earned everything else by a wide margin (US$99M for the year, versus about US$5M per month for the runner-up), so "top-grossing in Brazil" is essentially a list of global platforms and streaming services. The only local brand seen in the revenue lists is Globoplay.
- Mobile Time's revenue figures exclude anything billed outside the stores. Large Brazilian subscription businesses (exam prep, fitness video, finance) bill on the web by card, Pix or boleto and therefore do not appear in these charts; their absence is not evidence of low demand.
- If roughly US$100-135K per week is what the Health & Fitness leaders (Flo, Strava) earn in Brazil, a category leader makes on the order of US$5-7M a year there, and a mid-table app far less. This is a small prize compared with the same rank in the US.
- Duolingo's Brazilian annual price of R$179.90 against a US list price near US$84 per year implies a Brazilian price of roughly 35-45% of the US price, assuming an exchange rate of R$5-6 per US dollar (the exchange rate is an assumption, not sourced). ChatGPT's R$39.99 Go tier shows the same pattern: global publishers reach Brazilian payers with a lower tier, not with the US price converted.
- Government apps (Gov.br, CNH do Brasil, e-Título) repeatedly enter the top 10. Utility niches tied to public services therefore face a free official competitor, and demand spikes follow the public calendar (elections in 2026, licence-rule changes).

## 2. Brazil-specific niches: evidence of demand, existing apps and competition

### Takeaway
The niches with the clearest paid demand (public-exam prep, MEI administration, faith) are already served by large, well-funded incumbents or by free government apps, so "weak local competition" was not confirmed for any of them. Evidence was found for exam prep, CNH, MEI, faith and AI calorie counters; most of the other niches on the list could not be researched within the search limit.

### Cited Findings

**Concursos públicos (public-exam prep) — very large incumbents, billed on the web**
- Gran Cursos Online: gross revenue of R$465M in 2024 after 30% growth, with a target above R$550M for 2025 (self-reported; BTG as investor) — [Economic News Brasil, 30 Jun 2025](https://economicnewsbrasil.com.br/2025/06/30/gran-cursos-online/)
- Gran has 366 thousand students in its prep courses, has trained more than 1 million students in nine years, and its Gran Faculdade arm has 100 thousand distance-learning students (self-reported) — [NeoFeed](https://neofeed.com.br/blog/home/com-dinheiro-do-btg-gran-cursos-online-vai-prestar-vestibular-para-novas-areas/)
- Gran states a goal of 1 million students and half a billion reais in revenue using AI (sponsored content) — [Brazil Journal Brands](https://braziljournal.com/brands/como-o-gran-vai-usar-a-ia-para-chegar-a-1-milhao-de-alunos-e-meio-bilhao-em-receita)
- Qconcursos: about R$70M revenue in the year before Yduqs bought it in 2021; subscribers grew more than fourfold from 2014 to 2020, reaching 420 thousand — [Forbes Brasil, Jun 2021](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2021/06/yduqs-compra-plataforma-qconcursos/)
- A later article reports 460 thousand paying subscribers and about 2.1 million monthly users for the Qconcursos group, with a holding-company/IPO plan (article date not visible in the summary) — [Exame IN](https://exame.com/exame-in/qconcursos-holding-grupoq-ipo/)

### Inferences
- Exam prep is proven, large and local (one company near R$0.5 billion a year), but the leaders are web-first course businesses with teachers, content libraries and investor money. A solo developer cannot compete on content breadth; an opening would have to be a narrow tool (question drills for one exam board, spaced repetition, a specific exam such as OAB first phase) rather than "a concursos app".
- The CNH change created demand (the official app reached the national top 10) but also put a free official course and mock test in the same app, and at least five look-alike mock-test apps are already listed. This reads as a low-barrier, ad-supported, short-lived keyword play rather than a durable business.
- MEI tooling shows large user numbers for one private player (2M+), yet the government shipped a consolidated free app in January 2026 and free official invoice apps. The defensible space is what the state does not do (bookkeeping, cash flow, reminders, accountant hand-off), and it competes with fintechs that bundle these features.
- Glorify shows that a faith app can reach millions of Brazilian downloads, and that it took a venture-funded foreign team to do it. Another researcher's notes (`niches_opportunities.md`) record Glorify's revenue as largely ad-based, which fits a low-ARPU Brazilian audience.
- Low BRL price points from local entrants (R$9.90/month) suggest that Brazil-only consumer subscriptions are priced at about US$2 per month, far below US norms.

## 3. Brazilian success cases: indie developers, app studios, game studios, programmes

### Takeaway
Verified public numbers exist for Brazilian venture-backed or scaled companies (Moises with 70M+ users, Gran, Wildlife's hit games) but not for Brazilian solo app developers: three separate searches found no Brazilian indie publishing app revenue openly. Brazilian game studios that succeeded did so by exporting, and several were acquired by foreign groups (Aquiris by Epic, Oktagon by Fortis) or have shrunk (Wildlife).

### Cited Findings

**Brazilian-founded apps**
- Moises (AI music practice app; parent Music.AI) has more than 70 million users in over 190 countries. Brazil, the United States and Mexico lead by volume and together make up about half of the base. Brazil stands out in number of users, while the US generates more revenue because of higher conversion to subscriptions. Subscriptions are more than 90% of revenue; the enterprise side is smaller but growing (self-reported) — [Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/moises-amplia-escopo-e-avanca-como-hub-global-de-tecnologia-para-musicos/)

## 4. Company and tax practicalities for an app developer in Brazil

### Takeaway
Software development cannot be registered as MEI; the usual set-up is a micro-company (ME) in Simples Nacional under a software CNAE, taxed from 6% (Anexo III) if payroll including pró-labore is at least 28% of revenue, otherwise from 15.5% (Anexo V), plus roughly R$150-360 a month for online accounting. Apple pays into a Brazilian BRL account within 45 days of month-end with conversion by Apple's bank, and no source found settles whether App Store income from Brazilian users counts as export of services — a 2025 Receita Federal ruling on AdSense suggests it may not. An accountant must confirm all of this.

### Cited Findings

**Company type and CNAE (accounting-firm explainers)**
- CNAE 6201-5/01 (custom software development) is not on the list of activities allowed for MEI; the business must be set up as ME or EPP. CNAEs 6202-3/00 and 6203-1/00 are also marked "not allowed" for MEI in one table — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/lista-cnaes-desenvolvimento-software); [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/cnae-6201501-desenvolvimento-de-programas-de-computador-sob-encomenda-simples-nacional-fator-r-e-abertura-de-empresa/); [Contaja](https://contaja.com.br/blog/cnae-dev-profissional-ti/)
- Which CNAE: writing code for features, APIs, systems or apps for a client is 6201-5/01; 6201-5/02 is web design; 6203-1/00 covers standardized, non-customizable software — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/?p=4217); [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/lista-cnaes-desenvolvimento-software)
- Software CNAEs are subject to the Fator R: at 28% or more the company is taxed under Anexo III, starting at 6%; below 28% under Anexo V, starting at 15.5% — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/?p=4180); [Contabilidade Zen](https://www.contabilidadezen.com.br/blog/cnae-desenvolvimento-software-fator-r-2026/)
- Fator R is payroll of the last 12 months (including pró-labore) divided by gross revenue of the last 12 months — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/blog/gestao-fiscal/anexo-iii-fator-r-6-de-imposto/); [Valor Final](https://valorfinal.com.br/tabela-fator-r)
- The 2026 tables are unchanged from those set by LC 155/2016. Effective rate = (12-month revenue × nominal rate − deduction) ÷ 12-month revenue. In Anexo III the bracket R$720,000.01-1,800,000 has a 16% nominal rate with a R$35,640 deduction, and the top brackets (up to R$4.8M) are 21% and 33% nominal — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/tabela-anexos-simples-nacional-2026/)
- Sources disagree on wording: one says development "defaults" to Anexo V and Fator R can move it to III, another that it is III or V depending on Fator R with no default — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/?p=4180); [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/simples-nacional-para-empresas-de-tecnologia-cnae-anexo-e-armadilhas-comuns/)

**Export of services and how store income is classified**
- A Simples Nacional company may serve clients abroad and stay in the regime; when the operation qualifies as export, the Simples calculation leaves out the Cofins, PIS/Pasep, IPI, ICMS and ISS percentages on that revenue — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/melhor-contador-desenvolvedor-exterior); [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/empresa-do-simples-pode-prestar-servico-para-o-exterior/)
- The company is not fully exempt from the DAS: the portions of taxes without export relief remain — [Senhor Contábil](https://senhorcontabil.com.br/blog/como-receber-do-exterior-desenvolvedor-pj/)
- Export must be evidenced; one firm cites São Paulo's Parecer Normativo SF 04/2016 requiring a body of proof, and notes that no standard phrase on an invoice turns a domestic service into an export — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/melhor-contador-desenvolvedor-exterior)
- Solução de Consulta Cosit 185 of 19 Sep 2025 (Receita Federal): for Simples Nacional, revenue from showing videos and ads on the internet that are viewed in Brazil is **not** export of services, even when payment is intermediated by a platform based abroad and brings foreign currency into the country. The consulting party was a micro-company asking about Google AdSense receipts. The ruling is partially bound to SC Cosit 78/2019 and 31/2022 — [IBET](https://www.ibet.com.br/sc-185-2025-simples-nacional-exportacao-de-servico-nao-configuracao-hipotese-veiculacao-de-videos-e-anuncios-quando-visualizados-no-brasil/); full text: [APET PDF](https://rota.apet.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SC-Cosit-no-185-2025.pdf)
- By contrast, SC Cosit 73 of 10 Apr 2025 treated virtual assistance for a company domiciled abroad as export under Simples, applying the "result of the service" test — [IBET](https://www.ibet.com.br/sc-cosit-73-10-04-2025-simples-nacional-exportacao-de-servicos-assistencia-virtual-empresa-tomadora-domiciliada-no-exterior/)
- Accounting firms publish guides on declaring receipts from Google, Apple, Meta and Microsoft and on accounting for app developers, but none of the summaries stated how App Store proceeds from Brazilian end users are classified — [Senhor Contábil](https://senhorcontabil.com.br/blog/como-declarar-receitas-google-apple-meta-microsoft/); [Tactus](https://tactus.com.br/contabilidade-para-desenvolvedor-de-aplicativo)

**Accounting cost (vendor-published prices, 2026)**
- Agilize: online plans from R$259/month for service companies and R$359/month for commerce; its articles quote market ranges of R$199-899/month for ME/EPP in Simples (figures differ between its own articles) — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/preco-medio-contabilidade-mensal-microempresa); [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/comparar-precos-contabilidade-me-epp)
- Meu Contador Online: from R$149/month for service providers in Simples, depending on revenue — [Meu Contador Online](https://www.meucontadoronline.com.br/blog/quanto-custa-contador-pequenas-empresas/)
- Contabilizei is described (by a competitor) as pricing by revenue band; no starting price was found — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/comparar-precos-contabilidade-me-epp)

**Apple payouts (official Apple pages, read directly on 13 Nov 2026)**
- To be paid, a developer needs a Paid Apps Agreement in effect, banking information in App Store Connect, and to exceed the minimum threshold; "payments are made to the bank account and the currency you provided within 45 days of the last day of the fiscal month in which the transaction was completed" — [Apple, Overview of receiving payments](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/)
- Apple's bank consolidates proceeds from all sales currencies into one payment per fiscal month and "converts payment amounts into the currency of your bank account"; the rate is set by Apple's bank, typically the spot rate, and Apple says it "is generally a more favorable rate than used by your local bank" — [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/)
- "Payments may be subject to bank fees and transaction costs charged by your bank or intermediate financial institutions"; payments go by direct deposit/EFT, as low-value transfers where available; split payments to several accounts are not supported — [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/)
- Minimum payment threshold: for a bank in Brazil with a BRL account the threshold is US$0.02 (effectively none); bank country/currency pairs not listed in Apple's table must exceed US$40 — [Apple, Minimum payment threshold](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/minimum-payment-threshold/)
- Tax information for developers based in Brazil: Apple asks for the CNPJ ("a 14 character corporate tax ID"); all developers must also complete a US tax form, and non-US developers are routed to W-8BEN, W-8BEN-E or W-8ECI — [Apple, Provide tax information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/)

**Apple payouts in Brazil in practice (user-reported, Apple Developer Forums, read directly)**
- A Brazilian developer who chose BRL as bank currency received this notice from Apple (June 2022): "Due to regulatory requirements in Brazil related to BRL bank accounts, you need to complete and submit pre-trade documentation (either individual or corporate) to our bank's affiliate, StoneX", done online at banco.stonex.com with CPF or CNPJ, then printed, signed and mailed; payments "remain on hold until all required documents have been submitted to and approved by StoneX" — [Apple Developer Forums thread 708367](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/708367)
- In the same thread, developers report that Apple does not accept an intermediary bank and that payouts sent via an online remittance service "keep being returned" (Aug 2023); no Apple reply is recorded. A forum search for "StoneX" returns only this thread, so there is no newer public confirmation of the process — [Apple Developer Forums thread 708367](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/708367)

**US tax forms and US withholding**
- Apple Finance Support replies relayed by developers say: "Since the App Store contracts are characterized as a 'sales/commission' agreement, as opposed to a 'royalty' agreement, there is no tax withholding for sales in the USA App and Book Stores", and "application sales by non-U.S. developers or publishers on the App Store or Book Store are not subject to U.S. tax withholding or reporting. Therefore, completion of Part III - Claim of Tax Treaty Benefits, is not required" and no US TIN is needed (relayed Sep 2020; secondhand) — [Apple Developer Forums thread 660025](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/660025)
- A 2022 relay of the same reply uses "generally no tax withholding" and adds that non-US residents and entities are not required to provide a US tax ID — [Apple Developer Forums thread 708842](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/708842)
- Apple's public help page does not state any US withholding rule for app sales; it only says which form applies — [Apple, Provide tax information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/)

**D-U-N-S (official Apple page, read directly)**
- Companies must provide a D-U-N-S Number registered to the legal entity; individuals do not need one. D&B issues it free through Apple's look-up tool. Allow up to 5 business days to receive the number from D&B, then up to 2 business days for Apple to receive the data; expediting does not shorten the wait — [Apple, D-U-N-S Number](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/)
- The business must be a recognized legal entity; DBAs, trade names and branches are not accepted, and a sole proprietorship should enroll as an individual. D&B may contact the applicant and ask for registration documents — [Apple, D-U-N-S Number](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/)
- In Brazil the D&B partner is CIAL Dun & Bradstreet — [CIAL D&B](https://cialdnb.com/duns-brazil); a Brazilian guide says to request it with the CNPJ and expect 5 to 10 business days — [O Bastidor guide (PDF)](https://media-stg.obastidor.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/01133035/Criacao-de-contas-lojas-de-Apps.pdf). A TabNews post explains where Brazilians get stuck in Apple Developer Program enrolment — [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/adevelop/inscricao-no-apple-developer-program-o-que-a-apple-realmente-exige-e-onde-as-pessoas-travam)

**Google Play payouts (Google help pages, via search summaries only)**
- "Brazilian law does not allow Brazilian Reais to be paid outside of Brazil. In order to be paid in Brazilian Reais, you must have a bank account located in Brazil." A tax "is required for all merchants that receive payouts in any currency other than Brazilian Real (BRL) and will be automatically withheld from developer payouts." Elsewhere Google says wire payouts are issued in USD and the receiving bank may convert them. The summary did not pin each sentence to a page — [Google Play Console Help, wire transfer payouts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/2700656?hl=en); [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9023898?hl=pt-BR)

### Inferences
- With no employees, the Fator R depends entirely on the founder's pró-labore: paying oneself at least 28% of revenue as pró-labore keeps the company in Anexo III (from 6%), at the cost of INSS and personal income tax on that pró-labore. This trade-off is the main thing to model with the accountant.
- Which CNAE fits an own-product app business (6203-1/00, non-customizable software, or 6201-5/01) matters for municipal ISS and for the store's paperwork; the sources describe 6201-5/01 for client work and 6203-1/00 for standardized software, which points to 6203-1/00 for a publisher of its own apps. This needs confirmation.
- SC Cosit 185/2025 was about ads, not app sales, but its reasoning (the effect occurs where the Brazilian user is, regardless of a foreign payer and foreign currency) could be applied by analogy to App Store or AdMob income generated by Brazilian users. A cautious reading: revenue from foreign storefronts is the part most defensible as export; revenue from the Brazilian storefront and from ads shown in Brazil probably is not. This is an interpretation, not a ruling, and is the single most important question to put to an accountant.
- Fixed running costs for a company set-up, from the sourced figures: accounting at roughly R$150-360 per month (about R$1,800-4,300 a year), a free D-U-N-S, and the Apple and Google programme fees. Opening as an individual (CPF) avoids the D-U-N-S and accounting fee but was covered, with its tax downside, in the first-pass notes.
- Apple's BRL payout involves a one-off StoneX documentation step and can be blocked until it is approved; planning two to three weeks of slack before the first payout is prudent. The evidence is a 2022-2023 forum thread, so the process may have changed.
- If the Apple Finance statements still hold, a Brazilian developer's US-storefront sales suffer no US withholding even though Brazil has no tax treaty with the US, unlike YouTube/AdSense income.

### Gaps
- Nothing was found on Lucro Presumido rates for software, on Inova Simples (eligibility, limits, whether store income fits), or on the effective all-in rate on App Store income under each regime; the dedicated search returned only Simples Nacional tables.
- No source states how App Store proceeds are classified (export versus domestic; which Apple entity is the payer for Brazilian-storefront sales; what invoice, if any, is issued to Apple). SC Cosit 185/2025 was not read in full.
- A search summary mentioned "Resolução CGSN 191/2026", said to bring CBS and IBS into Simples Nacional from 1 Jan 2027; the source page was not identified, so this is unconfirmed.
- Not found: bank-side fees and IOF on Apple's BRL deposits, whether a USD account in Brazil or a foreign-currency fintech account is accepted, and current (2026) confirmation of the StoneX process.
- Not found: Google Play registration fee, D-U-N-S requirement for Google organization accounts, Google's payout threshold and timing for Brazil, and which tax Google withholds on non-BRL payouts.
- Google's own US-withholding treatment of Play app sales for Brazilian developers was not researched.
- **[background, unverified]** Leads to confirm with an accountant: Lucro Presumido for services is commonly described as roughly 13-17% all-in before export relief; Inova Simples is a simplified registration regime for startups with a low revenue cap; the Apple Developer Program fee is US$99 a year (one TabNews result cites about R$550 a year) and Google Play charges a one-time US$25.

## 5. Brazil-first versus global-first: evidence on monetizing Brazilian users

### Takeaway
The evidence found points the same way as the first pass: Brazil supplies users cheaply and at scale, but revenue per user is low and local price points are a fraction of US ones, so the Brazilian companies that scaled on the stores earn mostly abroad. Brazil-first is viable where the product is inherently local and can bill outside the stores (exam prep is the proof), or where a low BRL tier is used deliberately for volume.

### Cited Findings
- Moises: Brazil is the standout market by number of users, while the US generates more revenue thanks to higher subscription conversion; more than 90% of revenue is subscriptions (self-reported) — [Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/moises-amplia-escopo-e-avanca-como-hub-global-de-tecnologia-para-musicos/)
- Brazilians do pay for clear value at a local price: ChatGPT took an estimated US$99M through the stores in Brazil in 2025 (third-party estimate) — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/12/01/2026/apps-mais-baixados-br-25/); OpenAI moved to BRL billing and introduced a R$39.99 plan for Brazil — [Olhar Digital, 30 Oct 2025](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/10/30/internet-e-redes-sociais/chatgpt-lanca-plano-acessivel-no-brasil-cobra-em-real-e-fica-mais-barato/); [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/apps/chatgpt-plus-fica-mais-barato-no-brasil-com-nova-cobranca-em-real/)
- Seven in ten Brazilians have digital subscriptions, streaming leads, and 35% increased this spending in the last year (Vindi + Opinion Box "Pesquisa de Assinaturas 2025"; survey of internet users) — [Mercado & Consumo, 20 Aug 2025](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/20/08/2025/servicos/servicos-de-streaming-lideram-modelo-de-assinaturas-no-brasil/?noamp=mobile); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/servicos-de-streaming-lideram-assinaturas-no-brasil)
- Panorama Mobile Time/Opinion Box (survey of 2,309 Brazilian smartphone users, 11-27 Nov 2025): 97% have bought goods through apps or mobile sites, and the smartphone has replaced the wallet for 22% — [Mundo do Marketing](https://mundodomarketing.com.br/pagamentos-moveis-avancam-no-brasil-e-smartphone-ja-substitui-a-carteira-para-22-da-populacao)
- Undated older editions of the same survey series: only 17.6% said they had ever paid for an app, 47.8% had bought virtual goods inside apps, and the share paying for a video-streaming service rose from 38% to 45%. The summary gave no dates and did not say which Mobile Time article carries which figure; these may be several years old and should not be quoted without opening the pages. Candidate pages — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/?p=293713); [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/?p=618542); [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/?p=520806)

- Foreign short-drama publishers treat Brazil as a volume market: 24M monthly users in 2025 (Omdia; page attribution uncertain, see Section 1) and regional installs up 913% — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/apps-de-microdramas-crescem-913-na-america-latina)

### Inferences
- A workable rule from these cases: design the product for a global audience, treat Brazil as the cheap test and volume market with Portuguese localization and a manual low BRL tier, and expect most store revenue from the US and Europe. Moises is the direct precedent.
- Brazil-first makes sense in two situations seen in the data: (a) content or services that only exist in Brazil (public exams, MEI obligations, CNH, local faith content), and (b) products that can charge on the web via Pix/card and so escape both store commission and the store charts. The scale achieved there (Gran) came from content businesses, not from app-only products.
- The combination of a 27% iOS handset share (first-pass notes) with Android dominance in the revenue top 10 (four Google titles) means a Brazil-first consumer app should plan for Android and ads or very low prices; an iOS-only subscription app is structurally a global-first bet.
- Tax adds a further tilt: revenue from foreign storefronts is the part most likely to qualify for export relief under Simples Nacional, while Brazilian-user revenue may not (Section 4), so foreign revenue can be taxed more lightly as well as being larger per user.

### Gaps
- No Brazil-specific figures were found for trial-to-paid conversion, refund or chargeback rates, or churn, and no breakdown of iPhone ownership by income class (the 2025 Mobile Time/Opinion Box report has this but was not opened).
- No first-person accounts from Brazilian developers comparing Brazilian and foreign users' willingness to pay were found beyond Moises.
- No data on download or revenue uplift from localizing a global app into Brazilian Portuguese or Spanish was found; the planned search was dropped to stay within the limit.
- The June 2026 "Super Panorama" (Mobile Time/Opinion Box) covering generative AI, streaming and games behaviour exists but its numbers were not visible — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/17/06/2026/super-panorama-26/).

## 6. Community and learning resources in Brazil

### Takeaway
Active, verifiable communities exist for each stack: CocoaHeads Brasil (iOS/macOS; site, Slack and job board updated in 2026), Flutterando (the largest Flutter community in Latin America, active in October 2026), React Brasil and Android Dev BR (large but with little recent activity on GitHub). Conferences, podcasts and newsletters could not be verified because the one search on them returned nothing relevant.

### Cited Findings (GitHub pages read directly on 13 Nov 2026)
- CocoaHeads Brasil describes itself as the Brazilian Apple-developer community, with the site cocoaheads.com.br, links to upcoming events, a "be a host" page, Instagram, Bluesky and LinkedIn; its website repository was last updated on 13 Sep 2026 and its organization profile on 5 Aug 2026 — [GitHub, CocoaHeadsBrasil](https://github.com/CocoaHeadsBrasil)
- CocoaHeads Brasil's meetups are informal, with talks by local members or guests; the community runs a Slack with channels such as #code-help, #jobs, #design and #ux, and mentions a community podcast — [GitHub, CocoaHeadsSite](https://github.com/CocoaHeadsBrasil/CocoaHeadsSite)
- CocoaHeads Brasil keeps a job board for iOS and macOS developers (619 stars, updated 31 Aug 2026), searchable through openings.dev — [GitHub, CocoaHeadsBrasil/vagas](https://github.com/CocoaHeadsBrasil/vagas)
- Other CocoaHeads Brasil repositories: a Portuguese translation of "The Swift Programming Language" (last updated 2020), the equinociOS blog (2018), a repository of talks from NSBrazil 2019, a dataset of talks from chapters across Brazil (2019), and the association's bylaws (2018) — [GitHub, CocoaHeadsBrasil](https://github.com/CocoaHeadsBrasil)

### Inferences
- For an iOS-focused developer the practical entry points are the CocoaHeads Brasil Slack and events page (active in 2026) and MacMagazine/Mobile Time for market news; for cross-platform work Flutterando is the most active community by a wide margin.
- React Brasil's and Android Dev BR's GitHub activity has thinned since 2023-2024; their Slack groups may still be active, but this cannot be judged from GitHub.
- None of these communities is about the business of apps (pricing, ASO, paid acquisition). That discussion in Portuguese appears to happen ad hoc on TabNews and social networks, which matches the lack of Brazilian build-in-public cases in Section 3.
